Climate

Continental climate with cold winters and warm summers. The mountains nearby make weather change fast.

  • ☀️Best time: May to SeptemberWarm days from 22°C to 31°C. Ideal for mountain trips, parks and long walks.
  • 🥶Winter is properly coldDecember to February: highs near 0°C, lows to -9°C. Bring a heavy coat, hat and boots.
  • 🌧️Wettest in springMarch has about 13 rain days. April and May are also showery. A light rain jacket helps.
  • ⛰️Mountains are coolerHigher trails and the lake sit well above the city. Take a warm layer even in summer.
  • 🌡️Big day-to-night swingEvenings drop sharply, often 10°C or more. Carry a jacket for after sunset.
  • 🧳What to packLayers, comfortable walking shoes, sunscreen and sunglasses; winter boots and gloves from November to March.

Landmarks 9

Must-see · 8

  1. 1

    Kok Tobe Tower

    The city's most famous landmark, a TV tower on a hill above Almaty with sweeping views over the mountains and the city. Ride the cable car up from the city center, or take a taxi; go in the late afternoon for the best light and fewer crowds.

  2. 2

    Ascension Cathedral

    A striking wooden cathedral in Panfilov Park, famous for being built without nails and surviving earthquakes. It is an active church, so dress modestly and keep quiet inside; visit in the morning when it is quieter.

  3. 3

    Medeu

    A high-altitude mountain sports complex in a dramatic gorge, a classic Almaty excursion. Check opening times before you go and bring a warm layer, as it sits well above the city and is much cooler.

  4. 4

    Shymbulak

    A mountain resort reached by cable car from Medeu, popular for skiing in winter and hiking in summer. Go on a clear day for the best views and buy your cable car ticket at the base station.

  5. 5

    Big Almaty Lake

    A turquoise alpine lake set in the mountains above the city, one of the region's most photographed sights. Access can be restricted at times, so check locally before setting out, and go early to avoid afternoon traffic.

  6. 6

    Panfilov Park

    A leafy central park that holds the Ascension Cathedral and the Golden Warrior Monument, an easy and pleasant stop. It is free to enter and best visited on a mild morning combined with the nearby cathedral.

  7. 7

    Central State Museum of Kazakhstan

    The country's main museum, with collections on Kazakh history, archaeology and culture. Allow a couple of hours, and check the day's opening hours since museums here often close one day a week.

  8. 8

    Zelionyj Bazar

    A lively green market where you can see local produce, dried fruit, spices and dairy. Go in the morning when it is busiest and freshest, and bring small cash for purchases.

    Talghar (Talgar) · 31 min by road

    ★★★☆☆ · Full day · 31 km from Almaty

    Best way: The 31 km road trip is short, but transit needs a marshrutka plus a last-mile shared taxi, so a taxi is simpler and clearly better.

    Take a marshrutka (minibus) from Almaty's Sayran bus station toward Talgar, then a taxi or shared taxi up the valley road to the Turgen Gorge trailhead for the last few kilometres.

    • Car ★31 min · taxi ≈ 3,600 KZT

    What to see

    • Turgen Gorge hiking trails
    • Turgen Gorge waterfalls and river
    • Turgen Gorge horse riding area
    • Talgar town centre

    Good to know

    • Go in late spring to autumn; winter snow can make the valley road and trailhead difficult.
    • Taxi fare is 3600 KZT, so agree on the price before you set off.
    • Trailhead is for Turgen Gorge; plan for hiking and bring water and warm layers.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a day for Big Almaty Lake and the mountain road; go early and check road access on the day.
    • Swap one city day for Medeu and Shymbulak: cable car up, easy walks, cool air in summer.
    • History fans can add a half-day for the Central State Museum and the WWII memorial in Panfilov Park.

    Getting around

    • Metro: one line, open 06:00-00:00, 120 tenge per trip, contactless cards accepted at gates.
    • Buses: about 170 routes plus trolleybuses; cash fare 200 tenge, Onay card fare 120 tenge.
    • Onay card costs 800 tenge at караван newsstands; top up at Qiwi terminals, cash only.
    • Yandex Go is the main ride app; airport rides around 1500 tenge, but watch for fare scams.
    • Driving is busy and parking is hard; walking is easy in the centre, with gentle slopes toward the mountains.
    • Bikes and scooters are rentable by app; Yandex Go scooters run about 50 tenge per minute.

    Where to stay

    • Golden Quarter · ≈ KZT 35,000–90,000 a night
      Central and walkable, close to cafés, shops and the metro; easy base for first-timers.
    • Panfilov Park area · ≈ KZT 25,000–65,000 a night
      Next to the park, Zenkov Cathedral and the Green Bazaar; quiet streets and good value.
    • Abay Avenue · ≈ KZT 30,000–75,000 a night
      Wide avenue with hotels, restaurants and metro stops; a short ride to the centre.
    • Bostandyk · ≈ KZT 28,000–70,000 a night
      Leafy and calm, higher up toward the mountains; good for longer stays and families.

    Food & drink

    • Try Kazakh dishes: beshbarmak (meat with flat noodles), kazy and baursak bread.
    • Green Bazaar: buy dried fruit, nuts and horse-meat snacks; some stalls cook to order.
    • Lagman and plov at cheap cafeterias are filling; expect roughly 1500–3500 KZT a plate.
    • Coffee culture is strong in the centre; a flat white is often 1200–2000 KZT.
    • Book a table for dinner on weekends; popular places fill up. Check prices on the menu first.

    Tips

    1. Take the cable car to Kok Tobe for panoramic views of the city and mountains
    2. Visit Medeu and Shymbulak for world-class ice skating and alpine skiing
    3. Yandex Go is essential for getting around quickly and safely
    4. The mountains trap smog over the city during winter, so check air quality
    5. Try local delicacies like Beshbarmak (horse meat and noodles)
