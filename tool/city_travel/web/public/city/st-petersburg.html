Climate

Humid continental climate: cold winters, mild summers and frequent rain year-round.

  • ☀️Best time: May to SeptemberDays of 15–22°C and long evenings. June and July bring the White Nights.
  • 🥶Cold winters (December to February)Highs near -3°C to -1°C and lows to -8°C. Snow and ice on pavements; wear warm boots.
  • 🌧️Rain in every month10–16 rain days a month. July is wettest. Always carry a small umbrella.
  • 🌗Very short winter daysDecember daylight is brief; plan indoor sights and start early.
  • 🌉Bridge drawbridgesBridges rise at night from spring to autumn. Check the schedule before late trips.
  • 🧳What to packLayers, a waterproof jacket and comfortable shoes; a warm coat, hat and gloves in winter.

Landmarks 33

Must-see · 9

  1. 1

    Hermitage Museum

    One of the world's greatest art museums, housed in the Winter Palace, with vast collections from antiquity to modern art. Book tickets online to skip long queues, and go early on a weekday for fewer crowds.

  2. 2

    Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood

    A stunning cathedral with colorful onion domes and intricate mosaics inside, built on the site of Alexander II's assassination. Visit in the morning to avoid crowds; modest dress is recommended.

  3. 3

    Peterhof Palace

    A magnificent palace complex famous for its golden fountains and gardens, often called the 'Russian Versailles'. Take a hydrofoil from the city center in summer for a scenic arrival.

  4. 4

    St. Isaac's Cathedral

    A massive gold-domed cathedral with a richly decorated interior and a colonnade offering panoramic city views. Climb the colonnade for the best photo opportunities, especially at sunset.

  5. 5

    Peter and Paul Fortress

    The original citadel of St. Petersburg, housing the cathedral where Russian emperors are buried. Walk along the Neva walls for great views; the fortress is easily reached by metro.

  6. 6

    Nevsky Prospect

    The main avenue of the city, lined with historic buildings, shops, and cafes, perfect for a stroll. Start at the Moscow Railway Station and walk towards the Admiralty for the full experience.

  7. 7

    Mariinsky Theatre

    A world-renowned opera and ballet theater with a stunning interior. Book tickets well in advance for performances; the building is a short walk from the Sadovaya metro station.

  8. 8

    Bronze Horseman

    The iconic equestrian statue of Peter the Great, a symbol of the city, located on the Neva embankment. Visit at dusk when the statue is beautifully illuminated.

  9. 9

    Summer Garden

    A historic park with elegant fountains, sculptures, and shady alleys, ideal for a relaxing walk. Enter from the Neva embankment; the garden is free to visit.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a half-day to Peterhof by hydrofoil from the centre; check sailings and palace closures on the day.
    • Swap one museum for a canal boat ride: about 1 hour, best in the long evening light of June–July.
    • Take a day trip to Pushkin (Tsarskoye Selo) for the Catherine Palace; go early to beat the queues.

    Getting around

    • Metro is fast and cheap: about RUB60 per entry with a Podorozhnik card, about RUB86 by contactless card or token.
    • Metro runs roughly 05:45 to 00:15; stations are deep, so allow 1-2 min on escalators.
    • Buses, trolleybuses and trams cover the city; pay by Podorozhnik card or contactless.
    • Yandex Go (Yandex.Taxi) is the main ride app; local taxi firms also operate.
    • Bridges are drawn at night from spring to autumn; check the schedule before late trips.
    • Central sights are walkable; bikes are limited and winter cycling is hard.

    Where to stay

    • Nevsky Prospekt · ≈ RUB 4,500–14,000 a night
      Walk to the Hermitage, Kazan Cathedral and the main metro lines. Noisiest and busiest area.
    • Vasilyevsky Island · ≈ RUB 4,000–11,000 a night
      Quiet streets, the Kunstkamera and the Spit views. Two metro stops from the centre.
    • Petrogradsky · ≈ RUB 3,500–9,500 a night
      Green, local and cheaper. Close to the Peter and Paul Fortress and the zoo.
    • Kolomna · ≈ RUB 4,000–12,000 a night
      Calm historic streets near St Isaac's and the Mariinsky Theatre. Good for walkers.

    Food & drink

    • Try borscht, pelmeni and blini; lunch deals (business lunch) are much cheaper than dinner, often RUB400–800.
    • Pyshechnaya on Bolshaya Konyushennaya: cheap hot doughnuts and coffee, a local institution.
    • Book the famous restaurants ahead; walk-in queues are long on weekends.
    • Georgian and Uzbek food is popular and good value: khachapuri and plov, about RUB600–1200 a main.
    • Tipping is not required but 5–10% is welcome. Check if service is already added.
