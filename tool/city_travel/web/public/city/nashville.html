Getting around

Walk downtown; use rideshare or a rental car for everything else.

  • ✗MetroNo metro or tram. Don't plan around rail inside the city.
  • ✓BusMTA buses cover downtown and the airport. Route 18 links BNA to downtown hourly, about 35 minutes.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Taxis are common downtown. Uber and Lyft both operate, with dedicated airport pickup areas.
  • ✓Rental car ★A private car is the main way around. Highways run 55–70 mph but slow in traffic.
  • ✓WalkingDowntown is walkable. Outside it the city sprawls and sidewalks are patchy.
  • ✓TrainThe Music City Star commuter train runs from Riverfront Station to Lebanon. Useful for commuters, not most sights.
  • ✗FerryNo ferry service for visitors.
  • ✓Bike / scooterBikes and scooters work for short downtown hops.

Climate

Humid subtropical: hot, sticky summers with afternoon storms, and mild-to-cold winters. Spring and autumn are the sweet spots.

  • 🌤️Best time: April to May and September to OctoberHighs around 20–28°C with comfortable evenings. Ideal for walking downtown and outdoor music.
  • 🥵Summer is hot and humid (June to August)Highs near 30–32°C with muggy air. Do outdoor sights in the morning; rest indoors at midday.
  • ⛈️Summer stormsJuly and August bring the most rain days, around 15–17. Storms are often short but heavy; carry a small umbrella.
  • 🧥Winter is cold but shortJanuary highs near 9°C and lows around -1°C. A warm coat and layers are needed for evening walks.
  • 🌧️Rain is spread through the yearMost months see 9–17 rain days. Spring has more rain days than autumn, so pack a light rain jacket.
  • 🧳What to packLight breathable clothes and sunscreen for summer; a rain jacket; a warm coat and layers for December to February; comfortable shoes for walking downtown.

Landmarks 7

Must-see · 7

  1. 1

    Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum

    The definitive museum of country music, with gold records, stage costumes and instruments from the genre's biggest stars. Book tickets online in advance and allow at least two to three hours; it sits downtown near the honky-tonks.

  2. 2

    Ryman Auditorium

    The historic former home of the Grand Ole Opry, famed for its acoustics and pew seating. Check the schedule for concerts or take a daytime tour; it is a short walk from Broadway.

  3. 3

    Grand Ole Opry

    The long-running live country music radio show and a Nashville institution. Shows are at the Opry House outside downtown, so plan transport and buy tickets well ahead.

  4. 4

    Johnny Cash Museum

    A compact downtown museum devoted to the Man in Black, with memorabilia and personal items. It is popular and small, so go early in the day to avoid crowds.

  5. 5

    Belle Meade Historic Site

    A preserved plantation estate with a mansion, grounds and a winery, offering a window into the region's history. It is a drive from downtown, so allow half a day and check tour times.

  6. 6

    Cheekwood Estate & Gardens

    A botanical garden and art estate with seasonal displays and a historic mansion. Spring and autumn are the best times to visit; it is south of downtown and easiest by car.

  7. 7

    Vanderbilt University

    A well-known university campus with leafy grounds near the city center. It is a pleasant walk-through stop, especially combined with nearby neighborhoods.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a day for the Grand Ole Opry and a backstage tour; check show nights on the day.
    • History fans can swap an afternoon for The Hermitage, Andrew Jackson's plantation home, a short drive east of the city.
    • Families can add a morning at the Adventure Science Center or a walk in Centennial Park around the full-size Parthenon.

    Getting around

    • No metro or tram; the Music City Star commuter train runs from Riverfront Station to Lebanon.
    • MTA buses cover downtown and the airport; Route 18 links BNA to downtown hourly, about 35 min.
    • Taxis are common downtown; Uber and Lyft both operate, with dedicated airport pickup areas.
    • A private car is the main way around; highways run 55-70 mph but slow in traffic.
    • Downtown is walkable; outside it the city sprawls and sidewalks are patchy.
    • No ferry service for visitors; bikes and scooters work for short downtown hops.

    Where to stay

    • Downtown · ≈ USD 180–420 a night
      Walk to Broadway honky-tonks, the Country Music Hall of Fame and the riverfront. Noisier and pricier on weekends.
    • The Gulch · ≈ USD 170–380 a night
      Trendy and walkable, a short ride from downtown. Good restaurants and bars, easy highway access.
    • Music Row · ≈ USD 140–320 a night
      Quieter, near the studios and Vanderbilt. Close to midtown restaurants; a short drive downtown.
    • East Nashville · ≈ USD 130–300 a night
      Best value and the local food and bar scene. Five to ten minutes by car or rideshare to downtown.

    Food & drink

    • Try Nashville hot chicken: crispy fried chicken brushed with cayenne paste. Ask for the mild level first; the hottest is very hot.
    • Look for a meat-and-three: pick one meat and three vegetable sides, a Southern lunch staple, often under $15.
    • Broadway honky-tonks have live music most afternoons and evenings; many have no cover charge, but tip the band.
    • Brunch and biscuits are a local ritual; expect queues at popular spots on weekend mornings.
    • Prices vary widely. Budget about $15–25 for lunch and $30–60 for dinner per person, more downtown on weekends.

    Tips

    1. Visit the 'Honky Tonk Highway' on Lower Broadway for live music at any time of day
    2. Try Nashville Hot Chicken — it is famous for its intense, creeping spice
    3. Renting a car is essential if you plan to travel beyond the immediate downtown core
    4. The city has an incredible, rapidly growing food and craft cocktail scene
    5. Traffic can be very heavy on I-65 and I-40 during rush hours
