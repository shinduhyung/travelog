Getting around

Metro for Downtown and Marina; taxis or ride apps for everything else.

  • ✓Metro ★Red and Green lines link the airport, Downtown and Marina; fares AED 3–7.50 with a Silver nol card.
  • ✓BusBuses cover the rest of the city; tap out at the end of every ride.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Easy to flag; Careem and Uber both work in Dubai.
  • ✓Rental carDriving is common; rent a car with an International Driving Permit.
  • ✓WalkingFine in Marina and Deira but hard in summer heat; abras cross the creek for AED 1–2.
  • ✓AbraSmall wooden boats cross the creek for AED 1–2; the cheapest ride in the city.
Recommended stay★ 2 nights (3 days)

Two nights suits you: the old town, Burj Khalifa and Downtown are separate areas, so two days cover them without beach or desert trips.

Climate

Hot desert climate: sunny nearly every day, very little rain, and a long, humid summer.

  • ☀️Best time: November to MarchDays around 24–31°C with cool evenings. Ideal for beaches, souks and the desert.
  • 🥵Summer is extreme (June to September)Highs of 39–41°C with humid nights near 30°C. Go outside only early or after dark.
  • 💧Humid by the coastMuggy evenings from May to October; expect fogged sunglasses leaving air-conditioning.
  • 🌂Almost no rainOnly 0–2 rain days a month, mostly December to March. No umbrella needed most of the year.
  • 🌪️Occasional dustSpring and early summer can bring hazy, dusty skies; desert trips may be cancelled.
  • 🧳What to packLight breathable clothes, hat and strong sunscreen; a light layer for winter nights and cold AC; modest wear for souks and mosques.

Landmarks 25

Must-see · 8

  1. 1

    Burj Khalifa

    The world's tallest building and Dubai's defining sight, with observation decks offering sweeping views over the city and desert. Book timed tickets online in advance and go near sunset for the best light, though mornings are clearer.

  2. 2

    Dubai Mall

    One of the world's largest malls, packed with shops, dining and the Dubai Aquarium & Underwater Zoo inside. It sits at the foot of Burj Khalifa, so pair the two; evenings are liveliest and the metro stops right at the door.

  3. 3

    Palm Jumeirah

    The famous palm-shaped artificial island lined with beaches, hotels and marinas. Drive or take a taxi across it, and stop at the viewing points for photos of the fronds and the skyline.

  4. 4

    Dubai Fountain

    A choreographed fountain show set in the lake beside Dubai Mall and Burj Khalifa. Shows run in the evening; arrive early to grab a spot along the promenade for the best view.

  5. 5

    Burj Al Arab

    The sail-shaped luxury hotel that became a symbol of modern Dubai. It is best seen and photographed from the nearby beaches; access to the interior is limited to guests and diners.

  6. 6

    Museum of the Future

    A striking torus-shaped building housing exhibits on future technology and design. Book tickets ahead as slots fill up, and allow a couple of hours to explore the floors.

  7. 7

    The Dubai Frame

    A giant picture-frame structure with a glass skywalk linking two towers and views over old and new Dubai. Visit in the late afternoon for cooler temperatures and good light.

  8. 8

    Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood

    A preserved old-town quarter of wind-tower houses, courtyards and narrow lanes along Dubai Creek. Go in the morning to wander quietly, then take an abra across the creek.

    Swap in instead

    • Swap a mall afternoon for a desert safari: dune drive and dinner, roughly AED 150–350, book a licensed operator.
    • Take an abra across the creek (AED 1–2) and wander the Al Fahidi historic lanes and the souks.
    • Add a day trip to Abu Dhabi or Hatta; check bus and tour prices on the day.

    Getting around

    • Metro Red and Green lines link the airport, Downtown and Marina; fares AED 3-7.50 with a Silver nol card.
    • Nol Silver card costs AED 25 with AED 19 balance; a Red ticket suits up to 5 rides.
    • Buses cover the rest of the city; tap out at the end of every ride.
    • Taxis are easy to flag; Careem and Uber both work in Dubai.
    • Driving is common; rent a car with an International Driving Permit.
    • Walking is fine in Marina and Deira but hard in summer heat; abras cross the creek for AED 1-2.

    Where to stay

    • Downtown · ≈ AED 400–1,500 a night
      Walk to Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall; Metro Red Line on the doorstep.
    • Dubai Marina · ≈ AED 350–1,200 a night
      Beach and promenade at the door; Metro and tram nearby for the rest of the city.
    • Deira · ≈ AED 150–500 a night
      Cheapest beds, gold and spice souks, creek abras; Metro Green Line links Downtown.
    • Jumeirah · ≈ AED 500–2,500 a night
      Beach hotels and quiet streets; taxis needed for most sights.

    Food & drink

    • Try Emirati dishes: machboos rice, harees and luqaimat dumplings with date syrup.
    • Cheap eats in Deira and Bur Dubai: shawarma, karak tea and Indian cafeterias, roughly AED 10–30 a meal.
    • Fish and grills along Jumeirah Beach Road; book ahead for weekend brunch in hotels.
    • Alcohol is served only in licensed hotel bars and restaurants. Dress modestly in souks and malls.
    • During Ramadan, daytime eating is restricted in public; many hotels serve iftar buffets in the evening.

    Tips

    1. Dress modestly outside of beach/resort areas — cover shoulders and knees in malls and public spaces.
    2. Ramadan significantly changes dining hours; restaurants may be closed during daylight.
    3. Taxis are metered and affordable; Careem (regional Uber) is widely used.
    4. Dubai Mall and Mall of the Emirates are air-conditioned havens — free to enter.
    5. Alcohol is available in licensed hotels and restaurants but not in public; respect local laws.
