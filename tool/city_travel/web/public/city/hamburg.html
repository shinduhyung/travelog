Getting around

S-Bahn and U-Bahn for most trips; walk or cycle in the flat centre.

  • ✓Metro ★S-Bahn and U-Bahn cover the city. Buy a ticket before boarding; day tickets start around €8.20.
  • ✓BusBuses fill the gaps. Buy your ticket before boarding; no ticket sales on board.
  • ✓Taxi / ride appsMetered taxis are plentiful. Uber and Bolt work too.
  • ✗Rental carDriving is slow with roadworks and costly parking. Use park-and-ride if you must drive.
  • ✓WalkingThe city is flat and good for walking and cycling. Ferries cross the Elbe.
  • ✓FerryFerries cross the Elbe and are a cheap way to see the harbour.

Climate

Cool and changeable: mild summers, cold winters, and rain in every month. Pack layers and rain gear year-round.

  • 🌧️Rain all yearExpect 10–14 rain days every month. A light waterproof jacket beats an umbrella in wind.
  • ☀️Best time: June to SeptemberHighs around 20–23°C and long days. The busiest and most comfortable season.
  • 🧥Winter is cold, not extremeJanuary highs near 5°C and lows around 0°C. A warm coat, hat and gloves are enough.
  • 🌬️Wind off the ElbeHarbour and waterfront areas feel colder and windier than the city centre. Layer up.
  • 🌂Shoulder months are mild but wetApril, May, September and October are pleasant for walking, with frequent showers.
  • 🧳What to packLayers, a waterproof jacket, comfortable walking shoes and a warm hat for winter.

Landmarks 18

Must-see · 7

  1. 1

    Miniatur Wunderland

    The world's largest model railway, a must-see for its astonishing detail and scale. Book tickets online in advance to skip the queue, and visit early morning or late afternoon for fewer crowds.

  2. 2

    Elbphilharmonie

    Hamburg's iconic concert hall with a wave-like glass facade and a public plaza offering panoramic views. The plaza is free but requires a ticket; go at sunset for the best harbor views.

  3. 3

    Speicherstadt

    The historic warehouse district, a UNESCO World Heritage site with stunning brick Gothic architecture. Wander the canals and bridges on foot; it's especially atmospheric at dusk when the lights reflect in the water.

  4. 4

    HafenCity

    Europe's largest inner-city development, a modern waterfront district with striking architecture. Combine with Speicherstadt and Elbphilharmonie for a full day of exploration; use the U4 metro to get there.

  5. 5

    Reeperbahn

    The legendary entertainment mile in St. Pauli, famous for its nightlife and music clubs. Visit in the evening for the lively atmosphere, but be mindful of your surroundings; it's easily reached by S-Bahn to Reeperbahn station.

  6. 6

    St. Michael's Church, Hamburg

    A Baroque landmark with a towering spire offering superb views over the city and harbor. Climb the 132-meter tower for the best panorama; open daily, small entry fee for the tower.

  7. 7

    Hamburg Harbor Boat Tour

    A boat tour is the best way to appreciate Hamburg's maritime character and massive port. Choose a 1-hour tour from the Landungsbrücken; dress warmly as it can be breezy on the water.

    Swap in instead

    • Swap a city afternoon for a harbour ferry ride on the Elbe; regular ferries run and are cheap with a transit ticket.
    • Add a day trip to the Speicherstadt museums or the International Maritime Museum if you like ships.
    • Planten un Blomen park and the Wallanlagen are free and good for a slow half-day in dry weather.

    Getting around

    • S-Bahn and U-Bahn cover the city; no trams since the 1970s.
    • Buy tickets before boarding buses; no ticket sales on board.
    • Single tickets run about €2.10-12.60; day tickets from €8.20.
    • Ride apps like Uber and Bolt work; taxis are metered and plentiful.
    • Driving is slow with roadworks and costly parking; use park-and-ride.
    • The city is flat and good for cycling; ferries cross the Elbe.

    Where to stay

    • Altstadt · ≈ EUR 90–200 a night
      Central for the Rathaus, Kunsthalle and the lake. Walk to the main station and U/S-Bahn.
    • HafenCity · ≈ EUR 130–280 a night
      Next to the Elbphilharmonie and Speicherstadt. New hotels, quiet nights, short walk to the water.
    • St. Pauli · ≈ EUR 80–180 a night
      Nightlife, the Reeperbahn and the harbour edge. Lively and loud on weekends.
    • Altona · ≈ EUR 85–190 a night
      Local feel, good food, river views from the Elbe beach. Quick S-Bahn ride to the centre.

    Food & drink

    • Try a Fischbrötchen (fish sandwich) at the Landungsbrücken or the fish market; prices are roughly €4–8.
    • Franzbrötchen is the local cinnamon pastry. Buy one warm from a bakery for about €2–3.
    • Labskaus and Grünkohl are classic Hamburg dishes; look for them on traditional restaurant menus.
    • The Sunday fish market in Altona (early morning) is a Hamburg ritual; check opening hours on the day.
    • Book ahead for dinner on the Reeperbahn or in HafenCity on weekends; walk-ins get harder after 19:00.

    Tips

    1. Take ferry line 62 for a cheap and scenic tour of the massive harbor
    2. Visit the historic Speicherstadt, the world's largest warehouse district
    3. The Reeperbahn in St Pauli is the center of nightlife, but stay aware of your surroundings
    4. Carry an umbrella as the weather (Schietwetter) can be notoriously rainy and unpredictable
    5. Eat a traditional Fischbrötchen (fish sandwich) at the Sunday morning Altona Fish Market
