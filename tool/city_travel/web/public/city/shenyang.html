Getting around

Metro for central sights and Didi for everything else.

  • ✓Metro ★Lines 1, 2, 9 and 10 cover the city. Fares ¥2-5; trains run about 06:00-22:30.
  • ✓BusBuses cost ¥1-2 and take cash or Alipay/WeChat QR codes. Routes are hard to read without Chinese.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Taxis start at ¥8 for 3 km then about ¥1.8/km; insist the meter is visible. Didi works well and is easiest for foreign visitors.
  • ✗Rental carNot needed. Traffic and parking are hard for visitors; use metro and Didi instead.
  • ✓WalkingCentral sights in Shenhe are walkable. Beiling and Dongling need transport.
  • ✓TramTram line 2 links Taoxian Airport to the metro; the total trip downtown takes just over an hour.

Climate

A cold northeast China climate: freezing winters, warm humid summers, and the best weather in spring and autumn.

  • 🌸Best time: April to May and September to OctoberMild days around 16–24°C. Ideal for the palace, tomb parks and walking.
  • 🥶Winter is very coldDecember to February highs sit near -4 to 0°C with lows around -14°C. Bring a heavy coat, hat and gloves.
  • ☔Rain peaks in summerJuly and August have the most rain days, around 13–14. Keep a compact umbrella handy.
  • 🥵Short hot summerJune to August reach about 28–30°C and stay humid. Plan outdoor sights for morning.
  • 🌬️Spring can be windy and dustyMarch and April are drier but breezy. A light scarf helps.
  • 🧳What to packWarm layers and boots for winter; light clothes and rain gear for summer; a jacket for spring and autumn evenings.

Landmarks 3

Must-see · 3

  1. 1

    Shenyang Imperial Palace

    The only other imperial palace complex in China besides Beijing's Forbidden City, built by the early Qing rulers and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is smaller and far less crowded, so you can see the distinctive Manchu architecture up close. Go early on a weekday; tickets are sold at the gate and a passport is needed for foreigners.

  2. 2

    9.18 Historical Museum

    A powerful, sobering museum marking the 1931 Mukden Incident that began Japan's occupation of northeast China. The exhibits are extensive and mostly in Chinese, but the scale and artifacts speak for themselves. It is free with ID, closed Mondays, and best reached by metro plus a short walk.

  3. 3

    Shenyang Steam Locomotive Museum

    A specialist museum with a large collection of preserved steam locomotives, from huge freight engines to rare prototypes. It is a must for rail fans and an unusual stop for anyone curious about industrial history. Check opening days before you go, as hours can be limited, and allow an hour or two.

    Build my plan

    Swap in instead

    • Add a half-day for the 918 History Museum; it is large and sobering, so allow time.
    • Swap one city day for the Dongling tomb park if you have already seen Beiling.
    • In summer, take a slower afternoon in a park or teahouse instead of another museum.

    Getting around

    • Metro lines 1, 2, 9 and 10 cover the city; fares ¥2-5, trains about 06:00-22:30.
    • Tram line 2 links Taoxian Airport to the metro; total trip downtown just over an hour.
    • Buses cost ¥1-2 and accept cash or Alipay/WeChat QR codes; routes are hard to read without Chinese.
    • Taxis start at ¥8 for 3 km then about ¥1.8/km; insist the meter is visible.
    • Didi works well and is the easiest option for foreign visitors.
    • Central sights in Shenhe are walkable; Beiling and Dongling need transport.

    Where to stay

    • Shenhe (Old City) · ≈ CNY 250–600 a night
      Walk to the Imperial Palace and the old shopping streets. Best base for first-timers.
    • Taiyuan Street · ≈ CNY 220–550 a night
      Central shopping and food street, close to the Shenyang Station and easy metro links.
    • Shenyang North Station · ≈ CNY 180–450 a night
      Handy for high-speed trains and the metro; good value hotels and quick airport links.
    • Hunnan · ≈ CNY 200–500 a night
      Newer, quieter district with parks and the provincial museum area; needs transport to the old sights.

    Food & drink

    • Try Northeast classics: dumplings (饺子), guobaorou (sweet-and-sour pork) and stewed chicken with mushrooms.
    • Shenyang barbecue is a local ritual: grill at the table, order lamb skewers and cold beer.
    • Old streets near the Imperial Palace have snack stalls; prices are low, so pay in cash or by QR code.
    • Korean food is common and good here, thanks to the nearby border region; look for cold noodles and grilled meat.
    • Winter is hotpot season. Book popular places on weekends; expect queues after 6pm.

    Tips

    1. Visit the Shenyang Imperial Palace, the only existing royal palace in China outside of Beijing
    2. Bundle up heavily if visiting in winter; temperatures frequently drop well below freezing
    3. Try the hearty local Dongbei (Northeastern) cuisine, famous for dumplings and stews
    4. WeChat Pay and Alipay are mandatory for almost all payments
    5. The city has a massive Korean community, offering excellent authentic Korean BBQ
