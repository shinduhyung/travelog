Getting around

Delhi Metro for most trips; ride apps for late nights and the airport.

  • ✓Metro ★Cheap, air-conditioned and fast. Fares ₹10–60, more on the Airport Express. Nine lines; Yellow and Orange lines serve New Delhi station.
  • ✓BusDTC and Blue-Line buses are frequent and very cheap, ₹5–25, but crowded.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Uber and Ola work well. Use prepaid taxi booths at stations and the airport to avoid haggling.
  • ✓Rental carDriving is chaotic. Hiring a car with a driver is common for day trips.
  • ✓WalkingCentral New Delhi is fairly walkable with footbridges and subways. Use care crossing busy roads.

Climate

Delhi has hot summers, a wet monsoon from July to September, and cool, dry winters. October to March is the comfortable window.

  • 🌤️Best time: October to MarchMild days around 19–32°C and cool evenings. Ideal for forts, bazaars and long walks.
  • 🥵Summer is brutal (April to June)Highs of 36–39°C. Sightsee at sunrise, then stay indoors or in the Metro at midday.
  • 🌧️Monsoon (July to September)Up to 23 rain days in July. Short heavy downpours, humid air and slow traffic. Carry a light rain jacket.
  • 🌫️Winter fogDecember and January mornings can be foggy, which delays flights and trains. Build in buffer time.
  • 🧥Cold nights in winterJanuary lows near 8°C. Bring a warm layer for evenings; many hotels have weak heating.
  • 🧳What to packLight cotton for summer, a rain jacket for monsoon, warm layers for winter. Modest clothing for temples and mosques, plus comfortable shoes.

Landmarks 21

Must-see · 5

  1. 1

    Red Fort

    The Mughal emperor's red sandstone fortress and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the setting for India's Independence Day address. Go early in the morning to beat the heat and crowds, and note that foreign visitors pay a higher ticket than locals.

  2. 2

    Qutub Minar

    A soaring 73-metre victory tower from the 12th century, the tallest brick minaret in the world, surrounded by early Indo-Islamic tombs. Visit in the late afternoon for softer light; it is a short ride south of central Delhi.

  3. 3

    India Gate

    The grand war memorial arch at the end of Rajpath, best seen lit up after dark when locals gather on the lawns. Come in the evening; there is no entry fee for the surrounding area.

  4. 4

    Humayun's Tomb

    The garden tomb that inspired the Taj Mahal, a serene UNESCO-listed Mughal monument. Go at opening time for quiet paths and good photos; tickets are sold at the gate.

  5. 5

    Lotus Temple

    A striking flower-shaped Bahai house of worship open to all faiths, known for its calm silent hall. Entry is free but you must remove your shoes and keep quiet inside; it is closed on Mondays.

    Swap in instead

    • Day trip to Agra for the Taj Mahal: early train or a hired car with driver, roughly 3–4 hours each way. Check timings on the day.
    • Swap a city day for Humayun's Tomb, Qutub Minar and Lotus Temple in south Delhi, all on the Metro.
    • Add a half-day to Chandni Chowk spice market and the Red Fort, then rest in a haveli café.

    Getting around

    • Delhi Metro is cheap, air-conditioned and fast; fares ₹10-60, more on the Airport Express.
    • Metro runs 9 lines; Yellow and Orange lines serve New Delhi station.
    • DTC and Blue-Line buses are frequent and very cheap, ₹5-25, but crowded.
    • Ride apps Uber and Ola work well; use prepaid taxi booths at stations and the airport.
    • Driving is chaotic; hiring a car with driver is common for day trips.
    • Central New Delhi is fairly walkable with footbridges and subways; no ferries.

    Where to stay

    • Paharganj · ≈ INR 800–3,500 a night
      Cheapest beds, walk to New Delhi station and the Metro, and a short ride to Old Delhi. Busy and noisy streets.
    • Connaught Place · ≈ INR 3,000–12,000 a night
      Central, walkable and on the Yellow and Blue lines. Good for first-timers who want shops and cafés nearby.
    • Aerocity · ≈ INR 5,000–18,000 a night
      Next to the airport with the Orange line link. Best for short layovers or early flights, far from the sights.
    • Karol Bagh · ≈ INR 1,500–6,000 a night
      Mid-range hotels, a big shopping street and its own Metro stop. Lively and good value.

    Food & drink

    • Start with street chaat: golgappa, papdi chaat and aloo tikki. Pick busy stalls and eat freshly made food.
    • Old Delhi is the kebab and paratha heartland. Try a paratha lane breakfast and seek out a famous kebab shop.
    • Order butter chicken or dal makhani at a classic North Indian restaurant. Book ahead on weekends.
    • Vegetarian thalis are cheap and filling, often ₹150–400. Many places are pure veg.
    • Drink only bottled or filtered water, and skip ice and cut fruit from street carts if your stomach is sensitive.

    Tips

    1. The Delhi Metro is the absolute best way to travel long distances and bypass chaotic traffic
    2. Always negotiate auto-rickshaw fares before getting in, or ask them to use the meter
    3. Air pollution can reach severe levels, particularly between November and January
    4. Drink only sealed bottled water and avoid ice in drinks to prevent stomach issues
    5. Dress modestly, especially when visiting historical sites, mosques, or temples
