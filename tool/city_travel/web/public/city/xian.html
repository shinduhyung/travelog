Metro for most sights; walk or bike inside the city walls.

Climate

Four clear seasons: cold dry winters, hot humid summers with the most rain, and mild spring and autumn.

  • 🌸Best time: April to May and September to OctoberMild days around 19–28°C. Ideal for the city wall, the Terracotta Army and walking the old town.
  • 🥵Summer is hot and wet (June to August)Highs of 31–33°C with about 8–10 rain days a month. Go out early, carry water and an umbrella.
  • 🧥Winter is cold and dryJanuary highs near 6°C and lows around -4°C. Few rainy days; pack a warm coat and layers.
  • 🌧️Rain peaks in late summer and autumnAbout 10 rain days a month from July to October. Showers are often short; a light rain jacket helps.
  • 🌬️Spring can be dustyMarch to May sometimes brings dusty, hazy air. Check the air quality on the day if you have breathing issues.
  • 🧳What to packComfortable walking shoes, layers for cool mornings, sunscreen and a hat in summer, a warm coat in winter.

Landmarks 9

Must-see · 9

  1. 1

    Terracotta Army

    The buried army of thousands of life-size clay soldiers guarding China's first emperor is Xi'an's unmissable sight and one of the world's great archaeological discoveries. Go early in the morning to beat the tour groups, and book tickets online in advance since daily numbers are capped.

  2. 2

    Xi'an City Wall

    The most complete ancient city wall in China forms a 14 km loop you can walk or cycle on top of, with great views over the old town. Rent a bike near the South Gate in late afternoon and stay for sunset; the wall is lit at night.

  3. 3

    Big Wild Goose Pagoda

    This Tang-dynasty Buddhist pagoda, built to house scriptures brought from India, anchors a lively square with a musical fountain show in the evening. Climb to the upper tiers for city views, and dress modestly if you enter the temple grounds.

  4. 4

    Muslim Quarter

    A maze of lanes packed with street food, where the Hui Muslim community has traded for centuries. Come hungry in the evening for roujiamo and lamb skewers, and wander the side alleys away from the main tourist strip.

  5. 5

    Shaanxi History Museum

    One of China's best museums, with treasures from the Tang dynasty and earlier that give context to everything else you see in Xi'an. Entry is free but limited, so reserve a timed ticket online days ahead; it is closed on Mondays.

  6. 6

    Bell Tower of Xi'an

    The wooden tower at the exact center of the old city is a landmark you will pass constantly, and its upper gallery has fine views down the four main streets. Visit after dark when it is illuminated, and combine it with the Drum Tower opposite.

  7. 7

    Great Mosque of Xi'an

    A working mosque hidden behind a wall in the Muslim Quarter, blending Islamic function with Chinese courtyard architecture. It is an active place of worship, so dress respectfully and avoid prayer times; the entrance is off Huajue Lane.

  8. 8

    Huaqing Palace

    A hot-spring palace at the foot of Mount Li, famous for the Tang-dynasty love story of Emperor Xuanzong and Yang Guifei. It pairs naturally with the Terracotta Army on a day trip east of the city; go in the morning to avoid crowds.

  9. 9

    Daming Palace ruins

    The vast site of the Tang dynasty's main imperial palace, now a landscaped park with excavated foundations and a good museum. It is spread out and sunny, so bring water and shade; it is a quiet contrast to the busy city center.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a half day at the Shaanxi History Museum (book free tickets online in advance) and the Big Wild Goose Pagoda area.
    • Take a day trip to Mount Huashan by high-speed train for cable-car views; check weather and train times on the day.
    • Spend an evening at the Tang Paradise or a Tang-dynasty dinner show, or cycle the full city wall loop (rental about ¥45).

    Getting around

    • Metro: 12+ lines, single ride about ¥5; buy a Changantong card or scan WeChat/Alipay.
    • Buses: ¥1-2 flat fare; line 610 (tourist 8) links station, Bell Tower and pagodas.
    • Taxis: cheap but hard to hail at rush hour and shift change; watch the meter.
    • Ride apps: DiDi works widely; also WeChat and Alipay ride-hailing.
    • Driving: not advised for visitors; traffic and parking are tough.
    • Walking and shared bikes are best inside the city walls.

    Where to stay

    • Bell Tower / City Walls · ≈ CNY 250–700 a night
      Central and walkable. Close to the Bell and Drum Towers, the Muslim Quarter and metro lines.
    • South Gate / Shuyuanmen · ≈ CNY 300–850 a night
      Quieter old-town lanes near the wall and the calligraphy street. Good for evening walks.
    • Big Wild Goose Pagoda · ≈ CNY 280–800 a night
      Near the pagoda, museums and the fountain square. Lively at night, a bit south of the walls.
    • Xi'an North Station · ≈ CNY 200–550 a night
      Best for early high-speed trains. Few sights nearby; use the metro to reach the center.

    Food & drink

    • Eat in the Muslim Quarter: roujiamo (meat sandwich), yangrou paomo (lamb soup with bread) and grilled skewers. Prices are low; check the bill.
    • Try biangbiang noodles and hand-pulled noodles at small local shops. A bowl is often ¥15–35.
    • Dumpling banquets are a Xi'an classic. Book ahead for dinner shows; expect ¥80–200 per person.
    • Breakfast like a local: jinggao, hulatang (pepper soup) and sesame flatbreads from street stalls.
    • Tap water is not for drinking. Drink bottled or boiled water, and be careful with very spicy or cold street food if your stomach is sensitive.

    Tips

    1. Rent a bicycle to ride the entire 13.7km perimeter on top of the massive ancient City Wall
    2. Visit the Muslim Quarter (Huimin Jie) for incredible street food like Roujiamo (Chinese hamburger) and Yangrou Paomo
    3. The Terracotta Army is located about an hour outside the city; book tickets and a guide in advance
    4. WeChat Pay and Alipay are absolutely essential for daily life
    5. Smog can be heavy during the winter months, so check air quality index (AQI) reports
