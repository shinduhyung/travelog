Getting around

Metro for most trips; Didi or taxi for gardens and evenings.

  • ✓Metro ★9 lines; lines 1 and 4 cross downtown. Fares ¥2–6. Fast and cheap for the old town and the station.
  • ✓BusBuses run 05:00–21:00 every 10–20 min. Most fares ¥1–2; exact change needed. Tourist buses Y1–Y5 link the railway station and main sights.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Taxis flag ¥10 for 3 km, then about ¥3.2/km. Use the queue, not touts. Didi works well; few drivers speak English, so show Chinese addresses.
  • ✗Rental carNot worth it for a short visit. Traffic and parking in the old town are hard; use metro and taxis instead.
  • ✓WalkingFine downtown and along the canals. Bikes rent for about ¥30/day; keep bikes locked.

Climate

Humid subtropical: hot, wet summers and cool, fairly dry winters. Spring and autumn are the best times to visit.

  • 🌸Best time: April to May and October to NovemberHighs of 21–26°C and 23°C in October. Comfortable for gardens and canal walks.
  • 🥵Summer is hot and humid (June to August)Highs of 28–32°C with 14–16 rain days. Sightsee early morning; rest indoors at midday.
  • 🌧️Rain peaks in June and JulyAbout 16 rain days each month. Pack a light raincoat and shoes that dry fast.
  • ❄️Winter is cool, not freezingJanuary highs near 9°C and lows near 1°C. Rarely snows, but damp cold feels sharp; bring a warm layer.
  • ☂️Spring rainMarch and April each have about 11 rain days. An umbrella is worth carrying.
  • 🧳What to packLayers for spring and autumn, a warm coat for December to February, and rain gear for summer. Comfortable shoes for garden paths.

Landmarks 9

Must-see · 8

  1. 1

    Humble Administrator's Garden

    The largest and most celebrated classical garden in Suzhou, a UNESCO-listed masterpiece of pavilions, ponds and borrowed scenery. Go early in the morning to beat the crowds, and book tickets online in advance during peak season.

  2. 2

    Suzhou Museum

    A striking I. M. Pei-designed museum holding fine ceramics, calligraphy and local art, itself worth the visit for its architecture. Entry is free but timed reservations are required, so book ahead and avoid weekends.

  3. 3

    Tiger Hill, Suzhou

    A historic park crowned by the leaning Yunyan Pagoda, with legends of a buried king and sweeping views. Allow a couple of hours to wander the gardens and climb to the pagoda.

  4. 4

    Tongli Water Town

    A classic canal town of stone bridges, old lanes and boat rides just outside the city. Come on a weekday morning for quieter canals, and take a short boat ride to see it from the water.

  5. 5

    Pingjiang Road

    A preserved old-town street along a canal, lined with teahouses, small shops and traditional houses. Visit in the late afternoon for softer light and fewer crowds, and try a canal-side teahouse.

  6. 6

    Hanshan Temple

    A famous Buddhist temple tied to a beloved classical poem about its midnight bell. Dress modestly, keep quiet in the halls, and go early to enjoy the courtyards in peace.

  7. 7

    Lingering Garden

    Another UNESCO-listed classical garden known for its intricate rockeries and elegant halls. Pair it with the Humble Administrator's Garden on a garden-focused day and buy a combined ticket if offered.

  8. 8

    Grand Canal Boat

    A cruise along the historic Grand Canal shows Suzhou's water-town character from a different angle. Book a daytime or evening cruise in advance and bring a light layer for the breeze on deck.

    Build my plan

    Swap in instead

    • Add a day trip to Tongli or Zhouzhuang water towns: buses and trains run from Suzhou, about 1 hour each way. Check times on the day.
    • Shanghai is about 30 minutes by high-speed train. A half-day trip is easy from Suzhou Station.
    • Garden fans can swap one garden for the Master of the Nets Garden evening show, or add Tiger Hill and Hanshan Temple in one morning.

    Getting around

    • Metro: 9 lines; lines 1 and 4 cross downtown. Fares ¥2-6.
    • Buses run 05:00-21:00, every 10-20 min. Most fares ¥1-2; exact change needed.
    • Tourist buses Y1-Y5 link the railway station and main sights.
    • Taxis: flag ¥10 for 3 km, then about ¥3.2/km. Use the queue, not touts.
    • Ride apps: Didi works well. Few drivers speak English, so show Chinese addresses.
    • Walking is fine downtown; bikes rent for about ¥30/day. Keep bikes locked.

    Where to stay

    • Guanqian Street · ≈ CNY 250–600 a night
      Central shopping street; walk to the Humble Administrator's Garden and the museum. Busy and easy for first-timers.
    • Pingjiang Road · ≈ CNY 300–800 a night
      Canal-side lanes with teahouses and small inns. The most atmospheric base for evening walks.
    • Suzhou Industrial Park · ≈ CNY 350–900 a night
      Modern hotels, the lakefront and easy metro. Good value and quiet, but 20–30 minutes from the old town.
    • Near Suzhou Station · ≈ CNY 180–450 a night
      Cheapest and best for early trains to Shanghai; short metro ride to the gardens.

    Food & drink

    • Try Suzhou classics: squirrel-shaped mandarin fish, braised pork belly and sweet red-braised dishes. The local taste is sweeter than most Chinese food.
    • Suzhou-style noodles for breakfast: a bowl of soup noodles with toppings costs about ¥15–30.
    • Teahouses on Pingjiang Road serve local green tea; expect ¥50–100 per person for tea and snacks.
    • Crab season is roughly October to November; hairy crab is pricey, so check the price per liang before ordering.
    • Street snacks in the old lanes: sweet rice cakes, osmanthus sweets and small dumplings, mostly ¥5–20.

    Tips

    1. The classic Chinese gardens like the Humble Administrator's Garden get very crowded; arrive early
    2. Take a boat ride through ancient water towns like Zhouzhuang or Tongli
    3. High-speed rail makes it a perfect, easy day trip from Shanghai
    4. Explore Pingjiang Road for traditional tea houses, snacks, and canal views
    5. Silk products are world-famous here, but bargain carefully if buying in tourist markets
