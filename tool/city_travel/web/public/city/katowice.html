Getting around

Trams for most trips; the centre is walkable and tickets are cheap.

  • ✗MetroNo metro in Katowice. Use trams and buses instead.
  • ✓BusZTM Metropolia buses cover the whole metro area. Same tickets as trams: 20-min 4.60 zł, 90-min or full-route 6.60 zł, day pass 12 zł. Validate on boarding.
  • ✓Tram ★Trams and buses share one ZTM ticket system across the metro area. Buy at kiosks, machines or POP points; drivers sell only the 90-min ticket, cash only. Inspectors fine unvalidated tickets.
  • ✓Taxi / ride appsUber and Bolt work well. Agree taxi prices beforehand or use an app.
  • ✓Rental carUseful for day trips outside the metro area. In the centre, trams and walking are easier.
  • ✓WalkingThe centre is walkable. Metrorower yellow bikes rent via app across the region for longer hops.

Climate

Temperate climate with cold winters and mild, wet summers. Rain falls year-round, so pack layers and an umbrella.

  • ☀️Best time: May to SeptemberHighs of 18–25°C make walking and outdoor sights comfortable. July and August are warmest.
  • 🥶Cold winters (December to February)Highs of 2–5°C and lows down to -3°C. Bring a warm coat, hat and gloves.
  • 🌧️Rain all yearExpect 10–14 rain days every month. July is the wettest at about 14 days.
  • 🌂Pack an umbrellaShowers are common in every season. A light waterproof layer helps in spring and autumn.
  • 🌡️Big swings in spring and autumnApril and October can be mild by day and cool at night. Dress in layers.
  • 🧳What to packComfortable walking shoes, a rain jacket and warm layers for winter. Summer needs only light clothes plus something for cool evenings.

Landmarks 4

Must-see · 4

  1. 1

    Spodek

    Katowice's most recognizable building, a flying-saucer-shaped arena that has become the symbol of the city and hosts concerts and events. See it from outside at dusk when it is lit up; the surrounding Market Square area is easy to reach on foot or by tram from the center.

  2. 2

    Silesian Museum

    A major museum of regional history and art built on the site of a former coal mine, with striking architecture set into the old shafts. Allow a few hours and check opening days before you go, as museums in Poland often close one day a week.

  3. 3

    Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial and Museum

    The former Nazi concentration camp, a sobering and essential memorial about an hour from Katowice. Book tickets in advance, expect a lot of walking outdoors, and dress respectfully for the site.

  4. 4

    Legendia (Silesian Amusement Park)

    Poland's oldest amusement park, a fun family break from the city's industrial sights. Go on a weekday in summer to avoid queues and check ride schedules before traveling there.

    Build my plan

    Swap in instead

    • Swap a day for Nikiszowiec: a historic miners' estate with red-brick houses and a small museum, reachable by bus or tram.
    • Add a trip to the Silesian Park (Park Śląski) with its zoo and planetarium, a short ride west of the centre.
    • History fans can spend a half-day at the Auschwitz-Birkenau memorial, about 1.5 hours away by road; book a tour or check transport on the day.

    Getting around

    • Trams and buses run by ZTM Metropolia cover the whole metro area with one ticket system.
    • A 20-min ticket is 4.60 zł, 90-min or full-route 6.60 zł, and a day pass 12 zł.
    • Validate your ticket on boarding; inspectors fine unvalidated tickets.
    • Buy tickets at kiosks, machines or POP points; drivers sell only the 90-min ticket, cash only.
    • Uber and Bolt work well; agree taxi prices beforehand or use an app.
    • The centre is walkable; Metrorower yellow bikes rent via app across the region.

    Where to stay

    • Rynek · ≈ PLN 180–420 a night
      Right on the main square. Walk to most sights, bars and restaurants; best for a short first visit.
    • Śródmieście · ≈ PLN 150–350 a night
      The wider centre around the Rynek and station. Good transport links and a wide choice of hotels.
    • Koszutka · ≈ PLN 140–300 a night
      Quiet, green and residential, a short tram ride from the centre. Good value for longer stays.
    • Ligota · ≈ PLN 130–280 a night
      Calm western district near parks and the Silesian Park side. Cheaper rooms; rely on trams to the centre.

    Food & drink

    • Try Silesian classics: rolada (beef roll) with kluski dumplings and modra kapusta (red cabbage).
    • Order a bowl of żurek (sour rye soup) or a hearty kotlet schabowy at a milk bar (bar mleczny) for a cheap lunch.
    • Look for Silesian sausage and krupniok (blood sausage) at markets and casual grills.
    • Katowice has a strong café and craft-beer scene around the Rynek and Mariacka street; prices are lower than in Kraków.
    • Vegetarian and vegan spots are easy to find in the centre; check menus on the day, hours vary.

    Tips

    1. The city has been named a UNESCO City of Music
    2. Visit the Spodek arena for its unique, iconic UFO-like architecture
    3. The industrial sites like the Nikiszowiec district are fascinating for photography
    4. It is significantly more affordable than Warsaw or Krakow
    5. Train travel to nearby Krakow is fast and frequent
