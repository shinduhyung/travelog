Getting around

Ride apps or a rental car for most trips; the free streetcar for downtown and Ybor.

  • ✗MetroNo metro or train inside the city. Don't plan around rail.
  • ✓BusHART buses cover Hillsborough County, about $1.75 local and $2.75 commuter express. PSTA buses serve Pinellas County and connect the airport to downtown Tampa.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Uber and Lyft operate. Taxis are metered; book by app or phone.
  • ✓Rental car ★Driving is convenient but rush hours (7:30–9:30AM, 3:30–7:30PM) are slow, especially on I-275.
  • ✓WalkingDowntown is walkable. Bikes and scooters work for short trips, but summer heat is strong.
  • ✓StreetcarTECO Line Streetcar is fare-free and links downtown to Ybor City, roughly every 20 min.

Climate

Subtropical and humid. Mild, dry winters and hot, stormy summers with heavy afternoon rain.

  • ☀️Best time: November to AprilHighs of 21–28°C with few rain days. Ideal for walking, beaches and outdoor sights.
  • 🥵Summer is hot and humid (June to September)Highs near 31°C and lows near 24–25°C. Plan outdoor time for early morning.
  • ⛈️Heavy summer rainJune to September brings 20–27 rain days a month, often short afternoon storms. Keep plans flexible.
  • 🌂Pack a rain jacket in summerStorms pass quickly but soak you. An umbrella or light rain shell helps.
  • 🌊Beach days are best in spring and fallApril, May, October and November give warm water with less rain. Check conditions on the day.
  • 🧳What to packLight clothes, a hat and strong sunscreen. Add a light layer for winter evenings and indoor AC.

Landmarks 7

Must-see · 6

  1. 1

    Busch Gardens Tampa Bay

    The city's most famous attraction, a large amusement park known for its thrill rides and animal exhibits. It is a full-day outing, so arrive at opening to beat the queues. Buy tickets online in advance for a lower price and to skip the ticket line.

  2. 2

    The Florida Aquarium

    A top family draw on the downtown waterfront with marine life exhibits and hands-on experiences. Go on a weekday morning for smaller crowds. It sits near the Tampa Riverwalk, so pair the visit with a walk along the water.

  3. 3

    Tampa Riverwalk

    A scenic waterfront path linking downtown's museums, parks and the aquarium. It is free and best walked in the cooler early morning or at sunset. Wear comfortable shoes and bring water, as the Florida heat builds by midday.

  4. 4

    Ybor City

    The historic old town district with brick streets and a lively evening scene. Come in the late afternoon to explore before dinner. It is a short ride from downtown, so plan transport back if you stay late.

  5. 5

    Tampa Museum of Art

    A downtown art museum on the Riverwalk with a strong collection and rotating exhibitions. Check opening days before you go, as many museums close one day a week. It is an easy stop while walking the Riverwalk.

  6. 6

    ZooTampa at Lowry Park

    A well-regarded zoo with a focus on wildlife and family-friendly exhibits. Visit in the morning when animals are most active and the heat is milder. Buy tickets online ahead of time.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a beach day: drive west to Clearwater or St. Pete Beach, roughly 40–60 minutes. Check traffic on the bridges.
    • Busch Gardens Tampa Bay fills a full day for thrill rides and animals. Check ticket prices and opening hours on the day.
    • Swap a city afternoon for the Florida Aquarium or a Tampa Bay history museum if it rains.

    Getting around

    • TECO Line Streetcar is fare-free and links downtown to Ybor City, roughly every 20 min.
    • HART buses cover Hillsborough County; local fare about $1.75, commuter express about $2.75.
    • PSTA buses serve Pinellas County and connect the airport to downtown Tampa.
    • Ride apps like Uber and Lyft operate; taxis are metered and best booked by app or phone.
    • Driving is convenient but rush hours (7:30-9:30AM, 3:30-7:30PM) are slow, especially on I-275.
    • Downtown is walkable; bikes and scooters work for short trips, but summer heat is strong.

    Where to stay

    • Downtown · ≈ USD 150–350 a night
      Walk to the Riverwalk, museums and arenas. Streetcar to Ybor is free.
    • Ybor City · ≈ USD 120–280 a night
      Historic cigar district with bars and Cuban food. Livelier at night, quieter by day.
    • Channelside · ≈ USD 140–320 a night
      Waterfront near the Florida Aquarium and cruise terminal. Short walk to downtown.
    • Westshore · ≈ USD 130–300 a night
      Closest to the airport and the big mall. Good for early flights and rental cars.

    Food & drink

    • Get a Cuban sandwich at a Ybor City lunch counter. Expect pressed pork, ham and pickles for roughly $10–15.
    • Try deviled crab and Spanish bean soup at old Ybor cafeterias. Budget about $10–20 a plate.
    • Gulf seafood is the local pick: grouper, stone crab in season and shrimp. Prices vary; check the day's catch.
    • Food halls and Riverwalk cafés are easy for mixed groups. Expect $15–25 a main.
    • Summer heat is strong. Drink water often and plan a long indoor lunch during afternoon storms.

    Tips

    1. Visit historic Ybor City, famous for its 19th-century cigar factories and vibrant nightlife
    2. Rent a car to explore the wider Gulf Coast beaches like Clearwater and St Pete
    3. The city hosts the annual Gasparilla Pirate Festival, a massive city-wide celebration
    4. Prepare for daily, intense thunderstorms during the hot summer months
    5. The Tampa Riverwalk is a beautiful, pedestrian-friendly way to see the downtown waterfront
