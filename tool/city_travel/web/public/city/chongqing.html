Getting around

Metro for most trips; ride apps for hills, stairs and late nights.

  • ✓Metro ★Fast, cheap and air-conditioned, with announcements in Mandarin and English. Lines 3 and 10 link the airport to the city.
  • ✓BusFrequent and cheap, but signs are mostly in Chinese. Useful for short local hops.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Taxis are yellow and metered; few drivers speak English, so show your destination in Chinese. DiDi works well; set up the app before you arrive.
  • ✗Rental carNot advised for visitors: steep, confusing roads and hard parking. Use metro and apps instead.
  • ✓WalkingExpect stairs and hills everywhere. Biking is not advised in the centre. Wear good shoes.
  • ✓FerryA short Liangjiang ferry crosses the river for about ¥10. A cheap way to see the skyline from the water.

Climate

Humid subtropical climate: mild winters, hot sticky summers, and rain in most months. Expect hills, stairs and haze.

  • 🌡️Best months: October to DecemberHighs of 12–22°C and fewer rainy days. Comfortable for walking the hills and riverfront.
  • 🥵Hot, humid summer (June to August)Highs of 29–34°C with high humidity. Move slowly, carry water and rest at midday.
  • 🌧️Rain in most monthsSpring and autumn are wettest, with 14–17 rain days. Pack a compact umbrella and grippy shoes.
  • 🥶Cool, damp winterJanuary highs near 11°C and lows around 6°C. Buildings can feel cold; bring warm layers.
  • 🌫️Haze and mistRiver mist and haze can hide the skyline. Views are clearest after rain or on cooler days.
  • 🧳What to packComfortable walking shoes for stairs and hills, a rain jacket, and light breathable clothes for summer.

Landmarks 9

Must-see · 4

  1. 1

    Hongya Cave

    A multi-storey stilt-house complex glowing with lights at night, it is Chongqing's most photographed sight and a must-see for its dramatic setting above the Jialing River. Go after dark for the full effect, and arrive before 7pm to avoid the worst crowds.

  2. 2

    Ciqikou Ancient Town

    A preserved old town of cobbled lanes, teahouses and snack stalls, perfect for sampling local flavours and picking up crafts. Visit on a weekday morning for a quieter experience; it is easily reached by metro Line 1 to Ciqikou station.

  3. 3

    Dazu Rock Carvings

    A UNESCO World Heritage site of Buddhist cliff carvings dating back centuries, offering a peaceful and awe-inspiring day trip from the city. Wear comfortable shoes for walking between the carvings, and allow at least half a day; buses run from Chongqing's main bus stations.

  4. 4

    Liziba Station

    A metro station where trains pass directly through a residential building, a quirky symbol of Chongqing's mountainous terrain. Ride Line 2 to Liziba and watch from the viewing platform below; it is free and quick to visit.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a Yangtze River Three Gorges day or overnight trip from the Chaotianmen docks; check schedules and prices on the day.
    • Visit Ciqikou old town for teahouses and snacks, or the Three Gorges Museum for regional history (check opening hours).
    • Take the Yangtze cableway across the river for views, then walk the south bank; queues can be long, so go early.

    Getting around

    • Metro is fast, cheap and air-conditioned; announcements in Mandarin and English.
    • Metro Line 3 and Line 10 link the airport to the city.
    • Buses are frequent and cheap but signs are mostly Chinese.
    • Taxis are yellow and metered; few drivers speak English, so show your destination in Chinese.
    • Ride apps like DiDi work well; have the app set up before you arrive.
    • Walking means stairs and hills; biking is not advised in the centre.
    • A short Liangjiang ferry crosses the river for about ¥10.

    Where to stay

    • Jiefangbei · ≈ CNY 250–900 a night
      The central peninsula: walk to Hongya Cave, the riverfront and the main metro lines. Best for first-timers.
    • Jiangbei · ≈ CNY 200–700 a night
      Across the river from the skyline; good views back at Jiefangbei and easy metro links. Slightly quieter nights.
    • Nanping · ≈ CNY 180–600 a night
      South bank, near the Yangtze and the cableway area. Cheaper rooms and a local feel; a short metro ride to the centre.
    • Shapingba · ≈ CNY 150–500 a night
      University district with cheap eats and a busy station. Good value if you plan day trips by train.

    Food & drink

    • Order Chongqing hotpot: a spicy bubbling broth with beef, tripe and vegetables. Ask for mild (微辣) if unsure; prices are often per dish, roughly ¥60–120 a person.
    • Try Chongqing xiaomian (small noodles) for breakfast or a cheap lunch, about ¥8–15 a bowl.
    • Snack streets and night markets are everywhere; Jiefangbei and the old town lanes are easy starting points. Pay by phone app or cash.
    • River fish and spicy chicken dishes are local favourites. Look for busy places with local diners.
    • Street stalls are cheap and tasty; drink bottled water and pick stalls with high turnover.

    Tips

    1. Bicycles are useless here; rely on the CRT monorail or your own two feet
    2. Prepare for intense spice; Chongqing hotpot is famous for its numbing Sichuan peppercorns
    3. Do not rely strictly on 2D maps, as the city operates on multiple confusing vertical levels
    4. Take the Yangtze River Cableway for incredible views of the dense, cyberpunk-like skyline
    5. Summer is brutally hot; it is known as one of China's 'Three Furnaces'
