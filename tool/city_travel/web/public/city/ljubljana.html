Getting around

Walk the centre; use city buses for longer hops and ride apps for late nights.

  • ✗MetroNo metro or tram. Don't plan around rail inside the city.
  • ✓Bus ★City buses use the Urbana card; buy it at kiosks or with the Urbana app.
  • ✓Taxi / ride appsTaxis are metered; ride apps like Bolt and Uber operate in Ljubljana.
  • ✓Rental carDriving is easy outside rush hour; parking in the centre is limited and paid.
  • ✓Walking ★The centre is very walkable and bike-friendly; bike share and rental shops exist.
  • ✓BoatNo ferry service; boats run on the Ljubljanica river for tours.

Climate

Continental climate with cold winters and warm summers. Rain is spread across the year, heaviest in May.

  • ☀️Best time: June to SeptemberWarm highs of 22–28°C and long days. Ideal for the river, parks and day trips.
  • 🌧️May is the wettest monthAbout 16 rain days. Carry a rain jacket and keep outdoor plans flexible.
  • ❄️Cold wintersJanuary highs near 5°C and lows around -3°C. Pack layers, a warm coat and gloves.
  • 🌦️Rain all yearExpect roughly 8–12 rain days most months. A compact umbrella helps any season.
  • 🥵Hot summer afternoonsJuly and August reach about 27–28°C. Sightsee in the morning and rest in the shade midday.
  • 🧳What to packComfortable walking shoes, a rain layer and a warm jacket for winter. Summer needs light clothes and sun protection.

Landmarks 9

Must-see · 7

  1. 1

    Ljubljana Castle

    The hilltop castle is the city's defining sight, with views over the old town and river. Walk up from the old town or take the funicular; go early morning or late afternoon for softer light and fewer crowds.

  2. 2

    Triple Bridge

    This trio of bridges by Jože Plečnik links the old town with the modern centre and is the city's most photographed spot. It is free and open at all hours, so visit at dusk when the riverside cafés light up.

  3. 3

    Prešeren Square

    The main square is the heart of the old town, framed by the Franciscan church and the Triple Bridge. It is a natural starting point for a walking tour; mornings are calmest.

  4. 4

    St. Nicholas Cathedral

    The city's main cathedral is known for its ornate baroque interior and bronze doors. It is free to enter outside service times; dress modestly and keep quiet during mass.

  5. 5

    Tivoli Park

    Ljubljana's largest park offers wooded paths and a formal promenade leading up to the castle hill. It is free and ideal for a morning walk; enter from Prešeren Square via the Jakopič promenade.

  6. 6

    Metelkova Mesto

    This former barracks turned autonomous art and culture zone is covered in murals and street art. Visit in daylight for photos and check what is on in the evening; it is a short walk from the centre.

  7. 7

    National Museum of Slovenia

    The country's main museum holds archaeology and history collections, including the famous Ljubljana Marshes wheel. Check opening days before you go and allow a couple of hours.

    Swap in instead

    • Lake Bled is an easy day trip by bus or train (roughly 1–1.5 hours each way); go early to beat the crowds.
    • Postojna Cave and Predjama Castle make a full day out; book cave tickets ahead in summer.
    • Swap a city afternoon for a walk up Šmarna gora or a bike ride along the Ljubljanica for green views.

    Getting around

    • No metro or tram; the city runs on buses plus a small electric tourist train.
    • City buses use the Urbana card; buy it at kiosks or with the Urbana app.
    • Taxis are metered; ride apps like Bolt and Uber operate in Ljubljana.
    • Driving is easy outside rush hour; parking in the centre is limited and paid.
    • The centre is very walkable and bike-friendly; bike share and rental shops exist.
    • No ferry service; boats run on the Ljubljanica river for tours.

    Where to stay

    • Old Town (Stari trg) · ≈ EUR 90–200 a night
      Step out into the pedestrian lanes, the river and the market. Quiet at night, close to everything.
    • Center (Prešeren Square) · ≈ EUR 100–230 a night
      The most central base: bridges, buses and cafés on the doorstep. Some street noise.
    • Metelkova / Station · ≈ EUR 60–140 a night
      Cheapest beds, walk to the bus and train stations, and nightlife at Metelkova. A 15-minute walk to the centre.
    • Tivoli / Šiška · ≈ EUR 70–160 a night
      Green and calm next to Tivoli Park; good for families and longer stays. A short bus or walk to the centre.

    Food & drink

    • Try local dishes: štruklji (rolled dumplings), kranjska klobasa sausage, and potica nut roll for dessert.
    • The central market (Pogačarjev trg) has produce stalls and a covered market; some days bring street food and a morning food market — check on the day.
    • Riverside terraces along the Ljubljanica are the classic spot for a slow lunch or evening drink.
    • Cheap eats: burek bakeries and market snacks for a few euros; a sit-down lunch is often around €10–15.
    • Vegetarian and vegan options are common in the centre; book ahead for popular dinner spots on weekends.

    Tips

    1. The city center is largely pedestrianized and one of the most pleasant in Europe
    2. Take the funicular up to the castle for amazing views
    3. The Ljubljanica river banks are lined with vibrant, outdoor cafes
    4. Use the Urban card for public transit and bike-sharing
    5. Slovenia is a perfect base for day trips to Lake Bled
