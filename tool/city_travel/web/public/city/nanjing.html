Getting around

Metro for most trips; Didi or taxi for late nights and door-to-door rides.

  • ✓Metro ★Clean, cheap and fast. Single tokens cost ¥2–9 and run roughly 05:00–23:00. Line S1 links Nanjing South Station to the airport in about 30 min for ¥6.
  • ✓BusCity buses cost a flat ¥2 (some ¥1). An IC card gives about 20% off.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Taxis start at ¥9 plus a ¥2 service fee; most trips cost under ¥25. Didi works well, but drivers rarely speak English, so show Chinese addresses.
  • ✗Rental carNot needed in the city; metro, buses and taxis cover the sights. Skip it unless you plan trips outside Nanjing.
  • ✓WalkingGood in spring and autumn for Fuzimiao lanes, the city wall and lake paths. Hot and humid in summer, so walk early or late.
  • ✓Shared bikesHello and Meituan bikes are everywhere. Handy for short hops between metro stops and sights.
  • ✓TramTrams serve the southwest and Qilin Town. Useful only if your plans go that way.

Climate

Four clear seasons: cold, fairly dry winters and hot, humid, rainy summers. Spring and autumn are the best times to walk.

  • 🌸Best time: April, May and OctoberHighs around 21–26°C and comfortable lows. Ideal for the city wall, Purple Mountain and lake walks.
  • 🥵Hot, humid summer (June to August)Highs near 29–32°C with muggy nights around 21–25°C. Do outdoor sights early morning.
  • 🌧️Rain peaks in JulyAbout 17 rain days in July and 12–13 in June and August. Carry a light rain jacket or umbrella.
  • ❄️Cold winters (December to February)Highs around 8–11°C and lows near 0–1°C. Bring a warm coat, hat and gloves.
  • 🍂Dry, clear autumnOctober and November have fewer rain days and mild highs. Great for photos and long walks.
  • 🧳What to packLayers for spring and autumn, a warm coat for winter, breathable clothes and rain gear for summer. Comfortable shoes for cobbled lanes.

Landmarks 11

Must-see · 8

  1. 1

    Dr. Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum

    The resting place of modern China's founding father, set at the top of a grand 392-step stairway on Purple Mountain. It is the city's most iconic sight and draws huge crowds. Go early in the morning to beat the queues, and note the site is free but closed on Mondays.

  2. 2

    Confucius Temple, Nanjing

    A restored temple and lively pedestrian quarter along the Qinhuai River, best known for its evening lights and boat rides. Come after dark when the lanterns are on, and combine it with a stroll through the surrounding snack streets.

  3. 3

    Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall

    A sobering, deeply moving museum documenting the 1937 massacre, with powerful exhibits and architecture. It is free to enter but closed on Mondays; allow two hours and dress respectfully.

  4. 4

    Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum

    The tomb of the first Ming emperor, reached along a beautiful spirit way lined with stone animals. It sits on Purple Mountain and pairs naturally with the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum; a combined scenic-area ticket saves money.

  5. 5

    Nanjing City Wall

    One of the best-preserved ancient city walls in China, with walkable stretches and views over the old town and lakes. Walk a section near Zhonghua Gate in the late afternoon for cooler weather and good light.

  6. 6

    Presidential Palace

    A sprawling complex of gardens and government buildings that witnessed key moments of modern Chinese history. It is central and easy to reach by metro; book ahead online as tickets sell out on weekends.

  7. 7

    Purple Mountain

    The wooded mountain that anchors eastern Nanjing, dotted with tombs, temples and trails. Take the cable car or a taxi up, and set aside a full day to combine several sights on its slopes.

  8. 8

    Nanjing Museum

    One of China's largest and finest museums, with strong collections of art, history and porcelain. Entry is free with advance reservation, and it is closed on Mondays.

    Build my plan

    Swap in instead

    • Add a half day at the Ming Xiaoling tomb and Purple Mountain area; combine with the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum if you have energy.
    • Take a day trip by high-speed train to Suzhou or Yangzhou; check times and prices on the day.
    • Spend an afternoon at the Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall; it is sobering and needs quiet time. Check opening hours and booking rules on the day.

    Getting around

    • Metro is clean, cheap and fast; single tokens cost ¥2-9 and run roughly 05:00-23:00.
    • Line S1 links Nanjing South Station to the airport in about 30 min for ¥6.
    • City buses cost a flat ¥2 (some ¥1); IC card gives about 20% off.
    • Taxis start at ¥9 plus ¥2 service fee; most trips cost under ¥25.
    • Ride apps like Didi work well; drivers rarely speak English, so show Chinese addresses.
    • Shared bikes (Hello, Meituan) are everywhere; trams serve the southwest and Qilin Town.

    Where to stay

    • Xinjiekou · ≈ CNY 300–800 a night
      City centre. Metro interchange, malls and easy rides to most sights.
    • Fuzimiao · ≈ CNY 250–650 a night
      Confucius Temple lanes, river views and snack streets on your doorstep. Livelier and noisier at night.
    • Xuanwu Lake · ≈ CNY 350–900 a night
      Quiet, green and near the lake, city wall and railway station. Good for walks and slower evenings.
    • Nanjing South Station · ≈ CNY 250–600 a night
      Best for early high-speed trains and the S1 airport line. Fewer sights within walking distance.

    Food & drink

    • Try Nanjing salted duck (yanshui ya) and duck blood and vermicelli soup. Both are local staples.
    • Fuzimiao and Laomendong lanes are good for snacking: dumplings, tangbao soup buns and sweet rice cakes.
    • Look for small local noodle and duck shops off the main streets; a simple meal often costs ¥15–40.
    • Book a proper sit-down dinner for Jiangsu cuisine; expect roughly ¥60–150 per person, more in hotel restaurants.
    • Drink tea at a teahouse near the Confucius Temple area. Check prices first; tourist spots can be pricey.

    Tips

    1. Visit the massive Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum situated on the scenic Purple Mountain
    2. Walk or cycle along the remarkably well-preserved Ming City Wall
    3. WeChat Pay and Alipay are essential for all transactions
    4. Try Nanjing salted duck, the city's most famous culinary specialty
    5. The Memorial Hall of the Victims in Nanjing Massacre is a sobering but essential historical site

      Yunjin brocade

      CNY 40–1501 small piece · estimate

      Yunjin is a traditional silk brocade woven in Nanjing for centuries. Small items are often made from leftover fabric scraps.

      Where: Shop at the Nanjing Yunjin Museum
      Buy a small item made from fabric scraps. It is an easy and affordable way to take home real Nanjing silk.

      Yuhua tea

      CNY 80–25050 to 100 g · shop prices

      Yuhua tea is a green tea grown in the hills around Nanjing. It is known for its fresh, delicate flavor.

      Where: Official sales point of Nongcui (Tea Research Institute, Nanjing Agricultural University)
      Check the prices by grade before you buy. A 60 g box is a good size to try first.
