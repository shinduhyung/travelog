Getting around

Walk the centre and use the metro or tram for longer hops; get a Barik card.

  • ✓Metro ★Clean and fast. Single tickets cost about €1.70–1.95 by zone.
  • ✓Tram ★The green Euskotren tram runs along the river past the Guggenheim; single ticket €1.50.
  • ✓BusBilbobus city buses are red with a flat fare; Bizkaibus green buses serve the province.
  • ✓Taxi / ride appsTaxis are easy to find; ride apps like Uber and Bolt operate in Bilbao.
  • ✓Rental carDriving is hard: hilly one-ways, works, and a low emissions zone. Walk or cycle instead.
  • ✓WalkingBest for the old town and riverside. The centre is compact and walkable.

Climate

Oceanic climate: mild all year, rain in every month, and rarely very hot or cold.

  • 🌧️Rain any monthExpect 10–17 rain days every month. March and November are wettest. Always carry a small umbrella.
  • 🌡️Mild temperaturesHighs run from about 12°C in January to 26°C in August. Rarely freezing, rarely hot.
  • ☀️Best months: June to SeptemberWarmest and driest, highs 23–26°C with fewer rain days. Best for the river and the coast.
  • 🧥Layers, not heavy coatsMornings and evenings are cool even in summer. A light jacket and a rain layer cover most trips.
  • 🌫️Grey skies are normalCloudy days are common. Plan museums and pintxos bars as backup on wet days.
  • 🧳What to packComfortable walking shoes, a waterproof jacket and an umbrella. Sunglasses for summer.

Landmarks 8

Must-see · 7

  1. 1

    Guggenheim Museum Bilbao

    Frank Gehry's titanium-clad museum is the reason many people come to Bilbao, and the building itself is as much the attraction as the art inside. Book timed tickets online in advance, especially in summer, and go early in the day to see the exterior without crowds.

  2. 2

    Casco Viejo, Bilbao

    The old town's narrow streets hold the city's historic heart, with pintxos bars, small shops and the riverside nearby. Wander on foot in the late morning or early evening, and pair it with a stop at Plaza Nueva.

  3. 3

    Mercado de la Ribera

    This large riverside market is a lively spot for fresh produce and casual bites, and it sits right at the edge of the old town. Go in the morning for the fullest stalls, and check opening days before you visit.

  4. 4

    Zubizuri

    Santiago Calatrava's white pedestrian bridge is a photogenic link across the Nervión and an easy walk between the riverbanks. Visit at sunset for the best light, and wear shoes with grip since the glass walkway can be slippery when wet.

  5. 5

    Artxanda Funicular

    The cable car climbs to a hilltop viewpoint with a wide panorama over the city and river. Ride up in the late afternoon and walk back down or return by funicular before it closes.

  6. 6

    Bilbao Fine Arts Museum

    A strong collection of Spanish and European art makes this a worthwhile indoor stop, especially on a rainy day. It is a short walk from the Guggenheim, so the two fit easily into one day.

  7. 7

    Plaza Nueva

    This arcaded square in the old town is a classic spot for pintxos and people-watching. Come in the evening when the bars are busiest, and expect to stand at the counter like the locals.

    Swap in instead

    • Take the Artxanda funicular for a hilltop view over the city; a short ride and cheap ticket.
    • Day trip to the coast: train to Mundaka or Bermeo for fishing villages and the coast.
    • Swap a museum afternoon for the Bizkaia Bridge (Puente Colgante) in Getxo, a UNESCO site, by metro.

    Getting around

    • Metro Bilbao is clean and fast; single tickets cost about €1.70-1.95 by zone.
    • The green Euskotren tram runs along the river past the Guggenheim; single ticket €1.50.
    • Bilbobus city buses are red with a flat fare; Bizkaibus green buses serve the province.
    • A Barik card costs €3 and saves money on metro, bus, tram and local trains.
    • Taxis are easy to find; ride apps like Uber and Bolt operate in Bilbao.
    • Driving is hard: hilly one-ways, works, and a low emissions zone. Walk or cycle instead.

    Where to stay

    • Casco Viejo · ≈ EUR 70–160 a night
      Old-town lanes, pintxos bars and the cathedral on your doorstep; metro and tram nearby.
    • Abando · ≈ EUR 90–220 a night
      Central and handy: main train station, shops and a short walk to the Guggenheim.
    • Indautxu · ≈ EUR 80–190 a night
      Quiet, local feel with good restaurants; between the old town and the museum district.
    • Deusto · ≈ EUR 60–140 a night
      University area across the river; cheaper rooms and a short metro or tram ride to the centre.

    Food & drink

    • Eat pintxos standing at the bar: small plates on bread, €2–4 each. Point at what you want and keep your toothpicks for the bill.
    • The Mercado de la Ribera by the river is a good stop for fresh produce and casual bites; check opening days on the day.
    • Try bacalao (salt cod) dishes and txakoli, the local slightly sparkling white wine.
    • Lunch is the main meal; many restaurants offer a menú del día around €12–18 on weekdays.
    • Dinner starts late, often after 20:30. Bars are busiest Thursday to Saturday.

    Tips

    1. Eat 'pintxos' (Basque-style tapas) in the Casco Viejo (Old Town) bars
    2. The Guggenheim Museum is the city's crown jewel; allow plenty of time to explore
    3. The Basque language (Euskara) is widely used alongside Spanish
    4. Take the funicular up to Artxanda for a panoramic view of the mountains and city
    5. The city is highly walkable and very safe for pedestrians

    EUR 10–20750ml · estimate

    Txakoli is a light, slightly fizzy white wine from the Basque Country. Bilbao sits in the Bizkaia region, where it is made.
