Getting around

Metro for most trips; Didi for late nights and the tea villages.

  • ✓Metro ★13 lines and about 270 stations; fastest way around town.
  • ✓BusExtensive network; K-prefixed routes are air-conditioned, fares about ¥2–3.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Metered taxis start at ¥11; drivers rarely speak English, so show your destination in Chinese. Didi works widely; Uber is not the norm.
  • ✗Rental carNot practical for most visitors; use metro, taxis and bikes instead.
  • ✓WalkingGood around West Lake and old streets; use metro for longer hops.
  • ✓BikesPublic share bikes cost about ¥1–2 per ride; bike lanes are common.
  • ✓Water busGrand Canal ferry from Wulin Gate to Gongchen Bridge, about ¥3, a few trips daily.

Climate

Humid subtropical: hot, wet summers and cool, fairly dry winters. Spring and autumn are the best seasons.

  • 🌸Best time: April and OctoberAround 22–23°C by day with moderate rain. Ideal for West Lake and tea villages.
  • 🥵Hot, humid summer (June to August)Highs near 29–33°C with many rain days. Go out early and rest indoors at midday.
  • 🌧️Rain peaks in JuneAbout 18 rain days. Carry a small umbrella and plan indoor sights.
  • ❄️Cool winters (December to February)Highs near 10–12°C, lows near 1–3°C. Rare snow, but damp cold feels sharp.
  • 🍂Dry, clear autumnOctober and November are the driest and clearest months for photos.
  • 🧳What to packLayers for spring and autumn, a rain jacket in summer, and a warm coat in winter. Comfortable shoes for lake paths.

Landmarks 8

Must-see · 3

  1. 1

    West Lake

    Hangzhou's iconic lake, a UNESCO site with causeways, pagodas and gardens. Best visited early morning to avoid crowds; free to walk, boat rides cost extra.

  2. 2

    Lingyin Temple

    One of China's most important Buddhist temples, set in a forested valley with ancient grottoes. Go early to avoid crowds; modest dress recommended; entry ticket required.

  3. 3

    Leifeng Pagoda

    Historic pagoda with panoramic views over West Lake. Visit near sunset for the best light; ticket required; elevators available.

    Best way: High-speed trains reach Shaoxing North in under an hour, faster than the bus and with easy onward metro or taxi to the old town.

    • Book high-speed train tickets ahead for weekend travel

    Deqing · 41 min by train

    ★★★☆☆ · Full day · 41 km from Hangzhou

    Best way: The train covers the 41 km in about 41 minutes, quicker than driving and a good base for the Moganshan shuttle.

    Take a high-speed train from Hangzhou to Deqing, then a local bus or shuttle up to the Moganshan mountain villages.

    • Train ★41 min · from $2-$8
    • Car34 min · taxi ≈ 110 CNY

    • Mountain hiking trails
    • Sword Pond waterfall area

    Good to know

    • You still need a local bus or shuttle from Deqing up to the Moganshan villages
    • Moganshan is busiest and priciest in summer; weekdays are calmer
    • Bring cash or a transit card for the local mountain shuttle

    Build my plan

    Swap in instead

    • Swap a lake day for a tea village walk in Meijiawu or Longjing, about 30–40 minutes by taxi.
    • Add a day trip to Wuzhen or Xitang water towns; check bus and train times on the day.
    • Take the Grand Canal water bus from Wulin Gate to Gongchen Bridge, about ¥3.

    Getting around

    • Metro: 13 lines and about 270 stations; fastest way around town.
    • Buses: extensive network; K-prefixed routes are air-conditioned, fares about ¥2-3.
    • Taxis: metered, start at ¥11; drivers rarely speak English, so show your destination in Chinese.
    • Ride apps: Didi works widely; international apps like Uber are not the norm here.
    • Bikes: public share bikes cost about ¥1-2 per ride; bike lanes are common.
    • Water bus: Grand Canal ferry from Wulin Gate to Gongchen Bridge, about ¥3, a few trips daily.

    Where to stay

    • West Lake (Hubin) · ≈ CNY 400–1,200 a night
      Walk to the lake in minutes. Best for first-timers and evening strolls.
    • Wulin Square · ≈ CNY 300–800 a night
      Central downtown. Metro, shops and the Grand Canal water bus nearby.
    • Hangzhou East Station · ≈ CNY 200–600 a night
      Cheapest beds and fast high-speed trains to Shanghai. Not near the lake.
    • Xixi / Yuhang · ≈ CNY 250–700 a night
      Quiet wetland parks and newer hotels. Far from West Lake; budget taxi time.

    Food & drink

    • Try Dongpo pork, West Lake vinegar fish and Longjing shrimp at a lake-view restaurant.
    • Cheap eats: noodle and dumpling shops near Wulin Square, about ¥15–30 a bowl.
    • Drink Longjing green tea in a tea house; prices range widely, so ask first.
    • Snack streets: Hefang Street for sweets and street food, busiest in the evening.
    • Vegetarian temples serve simple set meals; check opening hours on the day.

    Tips

    1. Alipay is absolutely essential, as Hangzhou is the headquarters of Alibaba and is virtually cashless
    2. Visit a traditional teahouse near West Lake to taste the famous Longjing (Dragon Well) green tea
    3. Rent a shared bicycle to explore the vast and scenic perimeter of West Lake
    4. Weekends at West Lake are extremely crowded; try visiting early in the morning
    5. The high-speed train to Shanghai is incredibly convenient but book tickets in advance during holidays
