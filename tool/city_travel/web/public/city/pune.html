Getting around

Use Uber, Ola or autos for most trips; the metro and buses are cheap for longer hops.

  • ✓MetroTwo lines: Purple (PCMC to Swargate) and Aqua (Vanaz to Ramwadi). Useful for crossing the city; check the route on the day.
  • ✓Bus ★PMPML buses reach most areas. Fares about ₹5–30; day passes cost ₹40–50. Cheap but can be slow.
  • ✓Auto-rickshaw ★Everywhere and handy for short trips. Insist on the meter; roughly ₹18 for the first 1.5 km.
  • ✓Taxi / ride appsUber and Ola work well across the city. Local radio taxis also operate.
  • ✓Rental carDriving is busy but manageable. Two-wheelers are the local norm; helmets are compulsory. Scooter rental is about ₹200–300 a day for day trips.
  • ✓WalkingFine in the old city and market areas in cooler months. Use rides for longer hops in the heat.

Climate

Tropical with a hot, dry spring and a strong monsoon from June to September. October to February is the most comfortable.

  • ☀️Best time: November to FebruaryHighs around 29–32°C and cool nights near 15–17°C. Ideal for forts, old-city walks and day trips.
  • 🥵Hot and dry: March to MayHighs reach 34–37°C with little rain. Sightsee early morning and evening; avoid midday sun.
  • 🌧️Monsoon: June to SeptemberMany rain days, up to 28 in July. Expect heavy showers; keep plans flexible and carry rain gear.
  • 🌿Green after the rainOctober still has some rain days but hills and gardens look lush. Good for nature trips.
  • 🧢Sun protectionEven in cooler months the sun is strong at midday. Hat, sunscreen and water help.
  • 🧳What to packLight cotton clothes, a rain jacket or umbrella in monsoon, and a light layer for cool winter evenings.

Landmarks 5

Must-see · 5

  1. 1

    Shaniwar Wada

    The fortified palace of the Peshwas is Pune's defining monument, famous for its massive gates and the stories tied to its ruins. Go early morning to avoid the heat and crowds, and note the entry ticket is small but paid.

  2. 2

    Aga Khan Palace

    A grand palace with serene gardens, best known as the place where Mahatma Gandhi was interned. It is a peaceful, must-see stop; visit in the cooler morning hours and allow an hour or two.

  3. 3

    Dagdusheth Halwai Ganpati Temple

    One of Pune's most beloved and visited temples, dedicated to Lord Ganesha and especially vibrant during festivals. Dress modestly, remove shoes, and expect queues; early morning is calmest.

  4. 4

    Sinhagad Fort

    A hill fort with sweeping views and a strong place in Maratha history, popular for a half-day trip from the city. Go early to beat the heat and traffic, and wear shoes with grip for the uneven paths.

  5. 5

    Pataleshwar Cave Temple

    A rock-cut cave temple carved from basalt, right in the middle of the city and a quiet contrast to the busy streets. It is quick to see; combine it with nearby central sights and visit in daylight hours.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a day trip to Sinhagad or Lohagad fort for views and a walk. Go early; check the road and weather on the day.
    • Spend a morning at the Aga Khan Palace and the nearby Gandhi-linked sights if you like history.
    • Escape the city heat with a half-day trip to a hill spot or garden outside town. Check travel time on the day.

    Getting around

    • Pune Metro has two lines: Purple (PCMC to Swargate) and Aqua (Vanaz to Ramwadi).
    • PMPML city buses reach most areas; fares run about ₹5-30 and day passes cost ₹40-50.
    • Auto-rickshaws are everywhere; insist on the meter, roughly ₹18 for the first 1.5 km.
    • Uber and Ola work well across the city; local radio taxis also operate.
    • Driving is busy but manageable; two-wheelers are the local norm and helmets are compulsory.
    • Renting a scooter or motorbike costs about ₹200-300 a day for day trips.

    Where to stay

    • Koregaon Park · ≈ INR 2,500–7,000 a night
      Leafy lanes, cafés and restaurants. Good for food and nightlife; a short ride to the centre.
    • Deccan Gymkhana · ≈ INR 2,000–5,500 a night
      Central and close to FC Road, old-city sights and the river. Easy for first-time sightseeing.
    • Camp · ≈ INR 1,500–4,500 a night
      Historic core with markets and cheap eats. Close to the railway station and MG Road.
    • Viman Nagar · ≈ INR 2,200–6,000 a night
      Closest to the airport, with malls and cafés. Best for early flights or short stays.

    Food & drink

    • Try misal pav for breakfast: sprouted beans, spicy gravy and crunchy toppings. Ask for less spice if unsure.
    • Order a Maharashtrian thali for a full plate: bhakri, sabzi, dal, rice and chutneys.
    • Pune is famous for its bakeries. Grab chai and a bun maska or a fresh baked good in the afternoon.
    • Street snacks like vada pav and sabudana khichdi are cheap and easy to find. Prices are low, often ₹20–80.
    • Look for Parsi and Irani café dishes, and end meals with shrikhand or puran poli when you see them.

    Tips

    1. Known as the 'Oxford of the East' due to its massive student population and numerous universities
    2. Use Ola or Uber for auto-rickshaws to ensure fair pricing
    3. Visit the Shaniwar Wada fort and Aga Khan Palace for a glimpse into Maratha and independence history
    4. The city has a very vibrant cafe and vegetarian food scene
    5. Traffic is intense; build extra time into your schedule for any road travel
