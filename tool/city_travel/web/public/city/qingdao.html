Getting around

Metro for most trips; ride-hailing apps for the airport and late nights.

  • ✓Metro ★Fares ¥2–8; trains run about 06:00–22:30. Line 8 links the airport to Qingdao North Railway Station.
  • ✓BusBuses cost ¥1, or ¥2 with air conditioning; up to ¥6 for far suburbs.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Didi and other apps work well; pick up at the Ground Transportation Center. Taxis are plentiful but hard to flag in peak summer — use an app.
  • ✓Rental carPossible, but city traffic and parking are a hassle. Check licence rules on the day.
  • ✓WalkingGood in the Old Town and along the coast. Cycling is hilly and risky; shared bikes are scarce.

Climate

Four clear seasons: cold dry winters, mild springs and autumns, and a hot, humid, rainy summer.

  • ☀️Best time: May to June and September to OctoberMild days around 20–26°C, good for beaches, old streets and coastal walks.
  • 🥵Hot, humid summer (July–August)Highs of 27–29°C with 12–13 rain days a month. Plan indoor stops and early outings.
  • 🌧️Rain peaks in midsummerJuly and August are the wettest; carry a compact umbrella and check forecasts on the day.
  • 🧥Cold winters (December–February)Highs of 4–6°C and lows near or below freezing. Bring a warm coat and layers.
  • 🌊Sea breezesThe coast stays breezy; a light windproof layer helps even in summer evenings.
  • 🧳What to packLight clothes and sunscreen for summer, a rain layer in July–August, and warm layers for winter.

Landmarks 8

Must-see · 8

  1. 1

    Tsingtao Brewery Museum

    The birthplace of China's most famous beer, where you can tour historic brewing halls and sample fresh Tsingtao. Go on a weekday morning to avoid crowds, and bring ID for the tasting.

  2. 2

    Zhanqiao Pier

    Qingdao's iconic 440-meter pier stretching into the sea, ending at a pavilion with great views of the coastline. Visit at sunset for the best photos, and walk there from the old town.

  3. 3

    St. Michael's Cathedral

    A striking Gothic-style church in the heart of the old town, popular for its architecture and hilltop setting. Dress respectfully, and note that a small entry fee applies.

  4. 4

    May Fourth Square

    A modern waterfront plaza famous for its red 'May Wind' sculpture and sea views. It's free and especially lively in the evening when locals gather.

  5. 5

    Badaguan Scenic Area

    A leafy neighborhood of historic villas from various European styles, best explored on foot. Come in spring or autumn for pleasant weather and fewer tourists.

  6. 6

    No. 1 Bathing Beach

    Qingdao's most popular sandy beach, close to the old town and ideal for a summer swim. Bring your own towel and go early to rent an umbrella.

  7. 7

    Laoshan Mountain

    A sacred Taoist mountain with hiking trails, temples, and coastal views. Allow a full day, wear comfortable shoes, and take the bus or cable car up.

  8. 8

    Qingdao Olympic Sailing Center

    The 2008 Olympic sailing venue, now a marina with waterfront promenades and cafes. Visit in the late afternoon for a stroll and sea breeze.

    Swap in instead

    • Take a day trip to Laoshan for mountain trails and coastal views; check bus and ticket details on the day.
    • Ride the coastal path and beaches north of the city if the weather is clear.
    • Spend an afternoon in the old German concession streets for architecture and cafés.

    Getting around

    • Metro fares ¥2-8; trains run about 06:00-22:30.
    • Metro Line 8 links the airport to Qingdao North Railway Station.
    • Buses cost ¥1, or ¥2 with air conditioning; up to ¥6 for far suburbs.
    • Didi and other ride-hailing apps work well; pickup at the Ground Transportation Center.
    • Taxis are plentiful but hard to flag in peak summer; use an app.
    • Cycling is hilly and risky; shared bikes are scarce.

    Where to stay

    • Old Town (Shinan) · ≈ CNY 250–700 a night
      Walk to the pier, Zhanqiao, St. Michael's Cathedral and the beer museum. Best for first-timers without a car.
    • May Fourth Square · ≈ CNY 350–900 a night
      Modern seafront, malls and easy metro. Good base for the Olympic sailing centre and night views.
    • Badaguan · ≈ CNY 400–1,100 a night
      Quiet leafy streets of old villas between two beaches. Romantic and close to the coast walks.
    • Laoshan · ≈ CNY 300–800 a night
      Mountain and coastal scenery, tea villages and quieter beaches. Far from the city centre; check transport on the day.

    Food & drink

    • Eat seafood by weight at markets and small restaurants; agree the price before cooking.
    • Try Qingdao dumplings (mackerel or prawn filling) and grilled squid from street stalls.
    • Drink cold Tsingtao beer; the annual beer festival runs in summer — check dates on the day.
    • Breakfast like a local: youtiao fried dough sticks and soy milk from corner shops.
    • Budget roughly ¥30–80 per person for a casual meal; seafood dinners cost more.

    Tips

    1. Visit the Tsingtao Brewery Museum to learn the history and taste fresh, unpasteurized beer
    2. Beer is famously sold on the street in clear plastic bags during the summer
    3. Explore the Badaguan scenic area to see beautiful European-style historical villas
    4. WeChat Pay and Alipay are absolutely essential for all payments
    5. Enjoy fresh, local seafood at the bustling Pichai Courtyard (Pichai Yuan)
