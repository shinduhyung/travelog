Getting around

Opal card or contactless tap for trains, ferries and buses; ferries are the scenic way to cross the harbour.

  • ✓Train ★Trains, metro and light rail run on one Opal card or contactless tap. Fast way in and out of the CBD.
  • ✓Ferry ★Ferries from Circular Quay are the scenic way to reach Manly, Taronga Zoo and Watsons Bay.
  • ✓BusBuses fill the gaps in the inner suburbs. Google Maps and TripView show live times.
  • ✓Taxi / ride appsTaxis and Uber, DiDi and Ola all operate. Taxis are easiest at ranks and the airport.
  • ✓Rental carDriving is busy and tolled on most motorways. Parking in the centre is costly.

Climate

Warm temperate climate: mild winters, warm summers and rain spread through the year. Rarely very cold or very hot.

  • ☀️Best time: December to MarchWarmest months, highs 25–27°C. Best for beaches and harbour days, but also the busiest and priciest.
  • 🌧️Rain all yearRain days run from 6 to 15 a month. March is wettest; August is driest. Showers often pass quickly.
  • 🧥Mild winters (June to August)Highs around 17–18°C and lows near 8–9°C. Bring a warm layer; beach swims are for the brave.
  • 🌊Sea and sunSummer sun is strong; use sunscreen and shade. Check flags and surf conditions at patrolled beaches.
  • 🌂Pack for changeable daysA light rain jacket and layers work in every month. Evenings cool down near the water.
  • 🧳What to packT-shirts and shorts for summer, a jumper and jacket for winter, comfortable walking shoes and a hat.

Landmarks 25

Must-see · 7

  1. 1

    Sydney Opera House

    The city's defining icon, a UNESCO-listed building with a sail-shaped roof right on the harbour. It is a must-see for the architecture and the waterfront setting alone. Tip: book a guided tour in advance, and come at sunset for the best light.

  2. 2

    Sydney Harbour Bridge

    The huge steel arch frames the harbour and pairs perfectly with the Opera House. Walk across it for free, or climb it for the famous view. Tip: the pedestrian walkway from The Rocks is the easiest way to get onto the bridge.

  3. 3

    Bondi Beach

    Australia's most famous beach, a wide curve of sand with strong surf and a lively promenade. Tip: take the train to Bondi Junction then a bus, and swim between the flags.

  4. 4

    The Rocks

    The historic old town at the foot of the bridge, full of sandstone lanes, markets and pubs. Tip: come on a weekend for the markets, and walk here from Circular Quay.

  5. 5

    Darling Harbour

    A busy waterfront precinct with restaurants, the aquarium and family attractions. Tip: it is an easy walk from the city centre or a short ferry ride, and evenings are liveliest.

  6. 6

    Royal Botanic Garden Sydney

    A large free garden on the harbour edge with lawns, trees and views to the Opera House. Tip: enter near the Opera House and follow the path to Mrs Macquarie's Point for the classic view.

  7. 7

    Taronga Zoo Sydney

    A zoo on a hillside across the harbour with native animals and skyline views. Tip: the ferry from Circular Quay is the most scenic way to arrive, and mornings are quieter.

    Swap in instead

    • Swap a beach day for the Blue Mountains: train from Central (about 2 hours each way) to Katoomba for the Three Sisters and bush walks.
    • Take the ferry to Taronga Zoo for harbour views and a half-day with animals.
    • Walk the Bondi to Coogee coastal path (about 6 km, 2 hours) and swim at the beaches along the way.

    Getting around

    • Trains, metro, light rail, ferries and buses run on one Opal card or contactless tap.
    • Ferries from Circular Quay are the scenic way to reach Manly, Taronga Zoo and Watsons Bay.
    • Buses fill the gaps in the inner suburbs; Google Maps and TripView show live times.
    • Taxis and Uber, DiDi and Ola all operate; taxis are easiest at ranks and the airport.
    • Driving is busy and tolled on most motorways; parking in the centre is costly.
    • The CBD is walkable; bikes and e-scooters suit the harbour foreshore paths.

    Where to stay

    • The Rocks · ≈ AUD 180–450 a night
      Old sandstone lanes, weekend markets and a short walk to the Opera House and Circular Quay ferries.
    • Circular Quay · ≈ AUD 250–650 a night
      Best base for first-timers: ferries, trains and the harbour icons on your doorstep.
    • Darling Harbour · ≈ AUD 200–500 a night
      Family-friendly: aquarium, maritime museum and flat walks; light rail and buses nearby.
    • Bondi · ≈ AUD 150–400 a night
      Beach mornings and the coastal walk; 20–30 minutes by train and bus to the centre.

    Food & drink

    • Try a flat white and a meat pie or a lamington for a cheap daytime bite.
    • Seafood by the water: fish and chips at Watsons Bay or Manly, or the Sydney Fish Market.
    • Asian food is excellent and good value: Chinatown, Haymarket and the Spice Alley laneway.
    • Book ahead for dinner in Surry Hills and Newtown; walk-ins are easier at lunch.
    • Alcohol is sold in bottle shops and licensed venues; many cafés stop serving coffee early afternoon.

    Tips

    1. Opal card (tap-on/tap-off) covers all trains, buses, ferries, and light rail.
    2. The Bondi to Coogee coastal walk is free and spectacular — allow 2–3 hours.
    3. Sun protection is essential year-round; UV index is very high even on cloudy days.
    4. Tipping is not mandatory but appreciated for great service (10% is generous).
    5. Grocery stores (Woolworths, Coles) have excellent ready-to-eat options for budget meals.
