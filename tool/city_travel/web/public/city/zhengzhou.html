Getting around

Metro for most trips; Didi for late nights and sights off the metro lines.

  • ✓Metro ★7 lines, fares ¥2–9. First trains around 06:00, last around 23:00 (the Chengjiao line ends at 22:00). Cheap and easy for the centre and the station.
  • ✓Bus¥1–2 per ride with an extensive network and BRT lines. Cheap but slower than the metro.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Taxis: flagfall ¥8 day, ¥10 night, then ¥1.5/km after 2 km. Meters are used but watch for cheating. Didi works widely; Uber and similar international apps do not.
  • ✓Rental carNot recommended for visitors: heavy traffic and complex rules. Use metro, taxis and Didi instead.
  • ✓WalkingCentral areas are walkable and shared bikes are available, but the city is spread out. Use the metro for longer hops.

Climate

Four clear seasons: cold, dry winters and hot, humid summers with the most rain in July and August.

  • 🌸Best time: April, May and OctoberHighs around 21–29°C and fewer rain days. Ideal for the museum, ruins and day trips.
  • 🥵Hot, humid summer (June to August)Highs near 31–33°C with the most rain days. Plan indoor sights at midday and carry water.
  • 🌧️Rain peaks in July and AugustAround 12 rain days each month. Pack a light umbrella and a rain jacket.
  • ❄️Cold, dry winter (December to February)Highs of 7–11°C and lows near or below freezing. A warm coat and layers are essential.
  • 🍂Clear autumnSeptember and October turn drier and milder, with highs of 21–28°C. Great for outdoor sights.
  • 🧳What to packLayers for spring and autumn, a warm coat for winter, light breathable clothes and rain gear for summer. Comfortable shoes for temple and ruins visits.

Landmarks 5

Must-see · 3

  1. 1

    Song Mountain

    A sacred Taoist mountain with dramatic peaks and historic temples, perfect for hiking and scenery. Wear comfortable shoes and bring water; the cable car saves time if you're short on energy.

  2. 2

    Henan Museum

    One of China's best provincial museums, with treasures from ancient dynasties and a famous collection of bronze and jade. It's free with ID, closed on Mondays, and a good rainy-day option.

  3. 3

    Yellow River Scenic Area

    See the mighty Yellow River up close, with viewpoints and a giant statue of the Yellow Emperor. It's a bit far from the city center, so allow half a day and check bus routes in advance.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a day for Shaolin Temple and the Pagoda Forest near Dengfeng. It is a long day trip; check transport and ticket times on the day.
    • History fans can take the high-speed train to Luoyang for the Longmen Grottoes. Book seats ahead.

    Getting around

    • Metro: 7 lines, fares ¥2-9, first trains ~06:00, last ~23:00 (Chengjiao line ends 22:00).
    • Buses: ¥1-2 per ride, extensive network, BRT lines available.
    • Taxis: flagfall ¥8 day, ¥10 night, ¥1.5/km after 2 km; meters used but watch for cheating.
    • Ride apps: Didi works widely; international apps like Uber not available.
    • Driving: not recommended for visitors due to traffic and complex rules.
    • Walking/bikes: city is spread out but central areas walkable; shared bikes available.

    Where to stay

    • Erqi Square · ≈ CNY 150–400 a night
      Old downtown core. Walk to the Erqi Memorial Tower, street food and the metro interchange. Best for first-timers on a budget.
    • Zhengzhou East Station · ≈ CNY 200–500 a night
      On the high-speed rail and metro. Handy for Shaolin Temple and Luoyang day trips, but few sights nearby.
    • Jinshui Road · ≈ CNY 250–600 a night
      Central business strip with malls, offices and mid-range hotels. Good metro links and plenty of restaurants.
    • CBD · ≈ CNY 300–750 a night
      Modern lakeside district around the big convention centre. Newer hotels, quieter nights, a short metro ride to the old centre.

    Food & drink

    • Try Henan staples: hui mian (thick lamb noodles), hu la tang (peppery soup) and steamed dumplings. A bowl costs roughly ¥10–25.
    • Erqi Square and the nearby Dehua Street area are good for Hui (Muslim) snacks and evening street food. Prices are low; check hygiene on the day.
    • Zhengzhou is famous for hui mian noodle houses. Ask your hotel for a local favourite rather than a chain.
    • Night markets and food streets are busiest after 18:00. Carry small cash and expect crowds on weekends.
    • Tap water is not for drinking. Bottled water is cheap (¥2–3) and sold everywhere.

    Tips

    1. Visit the nearby Shaolin Temple, the birthplace of Kung Fu and Zen Buddhism
    2. WeChat Pay and Alipay are absolutely essential
    3. Try Huimian (braised noodles), the signature local dish
    4. The massive Foxconn factory here earns it the nickname 'iPhone City'
    5. Summers are hot and winters are cold and smoggy
