Getting around

Walk the compact centre; use the free tram and buses for the airport, Gare and Kirchberg.

  • ✗MetroNo metro. The city is small and covered by tram and bus.
  • ✓Bus ★Buses 1–31 serve the city and most stop at Hamilius and Gare. Free to ride.
  • ✓Tram ★Tram T1 links the airport to the centre and Gare every 8 minutes. Free.
  • ✓Taxi / ride appsTaxis exist but are limited; Uber and Bolt operate. Handy for late nights and heavy bags.
  • ✓Rental carEasy driving, but centre parking is paid and scarce on weekends. Best for day trips outside the city.
  • ✓WalkingGreat option: the centre is compact and bike-friendly, though the Grund sits in a steep valley.
  • ✗FerryNo ferries; the country is landlocked.

Climate

Temperate and changeable: mild summers, cold winters and rain in every month. Pack layers and a rain jacket year-round.

  • 🌤️Best time: June to SeptemberHighs of 19–24°C and the most daylight. Ideal for the old town, valley walks and outdoor cafés.
  • 🌧️Rain in every monthAbout 10–15 rain days a month, most in November to January. A light waterproof beats an umbrella in wind.
  • ❄️Cold wintersDecember to February highs of 4–7°C and lows near 0°C. Snow is possible but not guaranteed; check on the day.
  • 🧥Layers are keyDays can swing from cool mornings to warm afternoons. Bring a jacket even in summer evenings.
  • ☀️Long summer daysJuly and August give late sunsets, good for evening walks on the ramparts and in the Grund.
  • 🎄Winter charmDecember is cold and wet but festive, with Christmas markets in the centre. Dress for damp cold.

Landmarks 10

Must-see · 5

  1. 1

    Grand Ducal Palace

    The official residence of the Grand Duke and the ceremonial heart of the capital, set right in the old town. It is a must-see for its elegant facade and changing of the guard. Visit in summer when guided tours inside are offered; check times in advance.

  2. 2

    Bock Casemates

    A vast network of underground tunnels carved into the cliffs, a UNESCO-listed highlight of the city's fortifications. The views over the valley are superb. Go early to avoid crowds and wear sturdy shoes for the uneven passages.

  3. 3

    Le Chemin de la Corniche

    Known as Europe's most beautiful balcony, this walkway follows the old ramparts above the Grund valley. It is free and best at sunset for photos. Start near the Bock and stroll toward the city center.

  4. 4

    Notre-Dame Cathedral, Luxembourg

    The city's main church, with a striking Gothic facade and a richly decorated interior. It is a calm stop in the old town and free to enter. Dress respectfully and visit on a weekday morning for quiet.

  5. 5

    Adolphe Bridge

    The iconic arched bridge spanning the Pétrusse valley, a symbol of the city. Walk across for great views and photos. Combine it with a walk through the valley park below.

    Swap in instead

    • Day trip to Vianden: castle and river valley, roughly an hour by train and bus; check connections on the day.
    • Add a half-day to the Moselle wine villages (Remich, Grevenmacher) by bus or car.
    • History fans can swap a museum for the American Military Cemetery in Hamm or the Schueberfouer fair if it is on.

    Getting around

    • Tram T1 connects the airport to the city centre and Gare, every 8 minutes, free.
    • Buses 1-31 serve the city; most stop at Hamilius and Gare, free.
    • Taxis are available but limited; ride apps like Uber and Bolt operate.
    • Driving is easy but parking in the centre is paid and scarce on weekends.
    • Walking and cycling are great; the city is compact and bike-friendly.
    • No ferries; the country is landlocked.

    Where to stay

    • Ville Haute · ≈ EUR 130–280 a night
      Inside the old town: walk to the Bock, casemates and Grand Ducal Palace. Quiet at night, few budget rooms.
    • Gare · ≈ EUR 90–180 a night
      Around the main station: most hotels, best value, free tram and buses to the centre in minutes.
    • Kirchberg · ≈ EUR 110–220 a night
      Modern business district with big hotels and museums; good for parking and the EU quarter.
    • Grund · ≈ EUR 120–240 a night
      The old lower town in the valley: pretty, quiet and close to nightlife; steep walk or lift up to the centre.

    Food & drink

    • Try Luxembourgish classics: judd mat gaardebounen (smoked pork with broad beans), gromperekichelcher potato fritters and bouneschlupp soup.
    • Look for a 'Kniddelen' or 'Mettwurst' lunch in the old town; portions are big, so share.
    • Place d'Armes and the Grund have the densest café and restaurant clusters; book dinner on weekends.
    • Cheap eats: bakery sandwiches, kebab and Asian lunch spots near the Gare, roughly EUR 8–15 a meal.
    • Wine and beer are local: try a Moselle wine or a Luxembourgish craft beer. Prices in the centre are high, so compare menus.

    Tips

    1. You do not need to buy tickets or tap a card for public transport — just hop on
    2. French, German, and English are widely spoken, alongside the local Luxembourgish
    3. Use the Pfaffenthal panoramic elevator for stunning views and easy travel between the upper and lower city
    4. The city is extremely safe, making it perfect for walking and exploring at any hour
    5. Cost of living and dining is very high, comparable to Switzerland or Central London
