Getting around

Metro and buses for the centre; taxis or Didi for longer trips.

  • ✓Metro ★Lines run across the city; fares are up to ¥6 depending on distance.
  • ✓Bus ★Local buses cost ¥1 per ride; bus 1 runs along Yingze Dajie.
  • ✓Taxi / ride appsTaxis start at ¥8 flagfall; expect around ¥15–25 for cross-town trips. Didi is the main ride-hailing app; Uber does not operate in China.
  • ✓Rental carDriving is busy and parking is tight; walking and buses are easier in the centre.
  • ✓WalkingWalking works well in the flat centre; bikes and shared bikes also work well on flat central streets.

Climate

Continental climate with cold, dry winters and warm summers; most rain falls in July and August.

  • 🌸Best time: April to June and September to OctoberMild highs of 17–26°C and fewer rain days. Good for temples and walking.
  • 🥶Winter is cold and dryDecember to February highs are only 3–7°C with lows below freezing. Pack a heavy coat.
  • 🌧️Rain peaks in July and AugustAbout 12 rain days each month. Carry an umbrella and plan indoor sights.
  • ☀️Summer is warmJune to August highs reach 28–30°C. Go out early or late and drink water.
  • 🍂Autumn is crispOctober highs near 17°C and November near 10°C. Bring a warm layer.
  • 🧳What to packLayers for spring and autumn, a heavy coat for winter, and rain gear for July and August.

Landmarks 5

Must-see · 5

  1. 1

    Jinci Temple

    Taiyuan's most celebrated ancient site, a sprawling temple complex with Song-dynasty halls, old cypresses and spring-fed ponds that draws visitors from across Shanxi. Go early in the morning to avoid tour groups, and allow at least two hours to walk the grounds. It sits southwest of the city centre; a taxi or local bus is the easiest way out.

  2. 2

    Twin Pagoda Temple

    The pair of brick pagodas is Taiyuan's best-known skyline landmark and a pleasant place to climb for views over the city. The site is compact, so pair it with a walk in the surrounding park. Visit in late afternoon for softer light and fewer crowds.

  3. 3

    Shanxi Museum

    One of China's strongest provincial museums, with superb bronzes, Buddhist sculpture and artefacts explaining Shanxi's long history. It is free with advance reservation, so book online before you go. Closed on Mondays; allow half a day.

  4. 4

    Taiyuan Ancient City Wall

    A rebuilt stretch of the old city fortifications that gives a sense of Taiyuan's historic core and is good for an evening stroll. Come near sunset when the lights come on. It is central and easy to reach on foot or by taxi.

  5. 5

    Yongzuo Temple

    A quieter Buddhist temple known for its distinctive architecture and brick pagoda, worth an hour if you have time beyond the headline sights. It is less crowded than the Twin Pagoda Temple. Check opening hours before setting out.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a day trip to Pingyao old town by train; check times and tickets on the day.
    • Swap one city day for the Shanxi Museum if you like history and artefacts.
    • Spend a half day at Jinci Temple and its gardens if you have not already.

    Getting around

    • Metro lines run across the city; fares are up to ¥6 depending on distance.
    • Local buses cost ¥1 per ride; bus 1 runs along Yingze Dajie.
    • Taxis start at ¥8 flagfall; expect around ¥15-25 for cross-town trips.
    • Didi is the main ride-hailing app; Uber does not operate in China.
    • Driving is busy and parking is tight; walking and buses are easier in the centre.
    • Bikes and shared bikes work well on flat central streets.

    Where to stay

    • Yingze Dajie · ≈ CNY 200–500 a night
      Main east-west avenue; bus 1 runs along it and many sights and shops are close.
    • Train Station area · ≈ CNY 150–400 a night
      Easiest for early trains and budget hotels; short taxi rides to the centre.
    • Jinci Road · ≈ CNY 180–450 a night
      Good base toward Jinci Temple in the southwest; quieter streets.
    • Wuyi Square · ≈ CNY 200–550 a night
      Central square with shops and food nearby; easy walking and buses.

    Food & drink

    • Try Shanxi noodles: knife-cut noodles (daoxiao mian) and hand-pulled noodles, often with a vinegar-based sauce.
    • Shanxi aged vinegar is the local pride. Look for vinegar shops and taste before you buy.
    • Breakfast: steamed buns, millet porridge and fried dough sticks at small street shops, cheap and filling.
    • Look for local wheat dishes like mao'erduo and kaolaolao; portions are large, so share.
    • Prices are low: a simple noodle meal is often around ¥10–30. Check menus for prices before ordering.

    Tips

    1. Shanxi is famous across China for its incredible variety of noodles and aged dark vinegar
    2. Visit the Jinci Temple for stunning ancient architecture and serene gardens
    3. WeChat Pay and Alipay are absolutely mandatory for daily life
    4. The city has largely shed its heavily polluted reputation, but check AQI during winter
    5. It serves as an excellent base camp for visiting Pingyao Ancient City and the Yungang Grottoes
