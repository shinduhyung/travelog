Climate

Cold continental climate: long freezing winters, short warm summers, and most rain from June to August.

  • ❄️Winter is long and harshDecember to February highs sit near -8 to -3°C, lows to -18°C. Pack boots, a hat and layers.
  • 🌸Best time: May and SeptemberAround 22°C by day, mild evenings and fewer rain days. Ideal for parks and walking.
  • ☔Rain peaks in summerJune to August bring 14–15 rain days a month. Carry an umbrella and plan indoor backups.
  • 🥵Short, warm summerJuly highs near 28°C with humid nights around 20°C. Go out early or after sunset.
  • 🍂Spring and autumn are shortApril and October are mild but changeable; March and November can still feel like winter.
  • 🧳What to packWarm layers and boots for November to March; light clothes and rain gear for June to August.

Landmarks 5

Must-see · 5

  1. 1

    Museum of the Imperial Palace of Manchukuo

    The former palace of Puyi, the last emperor of China, who ruled the Japanese puppet state of Manchukuo here. It is the city's most important historical site and a must-see for understanding 20th-century China. Tip: go on a weekday morning to avoid crowds; the site is a short taxi ride from the city center.

  2. 2

    Changchun Film Studio

    One of China's oldest and most famous film studios, now a museum dedicated to the country's film history. It is a must for cinema fans and anyone interested in Chinese culture. Tip: check opening hours in advance, as they can vary; it is easily reached by taxi or bus.

  3. 3

    Jingyuetan National Forest Park

    A large forest park with a lake, ideal for hiking, cycling, and escaping the city. It is the best place in Changchun to enjoy nature. Tip: visit in summer or autumn for the best scenery; bring water and snacks as facilities are limited.

  4. 4

    Changchun World Sculpture Park

    A unique park displaying sculptures from around the world, set in a pleasant green space. It is a relaxing and culturally interesting stop. Tip: go in the late afternoon to avoid the heat and enjoy a stroll.

  5. 5

    Nanhu Park

    A popular city park with a lake, gardens, and recreational activities. It is a great spot to experience local life. Tip: visit on a weekend to see locals enjoying the park; entry is free.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a half-day at Jingyuetan park: lake walks, forest trails and winter skiing; check opening hours on the day.
    • History fans can swap a shopping afternoon for the Film Museum and the old puppet-state buildings; check tickets on the day.
    • In winter, swap a park visit for an indoor museum or the hot springs outside town; check transport on the day.

    Getting around

    • Metro and light rail: 6 lines (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8) cover many central areas; T-Union card works.
    • Trams 54 and 55 run in the west; fare ¥2, route 55 ends at West station.
    • Buses are extensive and cost about ¥2; Chinese reading helps a lot.
    • Taxis are cheap and easy; flag fall ¥5 plus ¥1 fuel surcharge.
    • Ride apps: Didi works; local apps may need Chinese registration.
    • Walking is fine May to mid-November; cycling is risky in traffic.

    Where to stay

    • People's Square · ≈ CNY 200–550 a night
      Central and walkable; metro lines 1 and 2 meet here, so most sights are a short ride away.
    • Chongqing Road · ≈ CNY 180–500 a night
      Shopping and food street; busy at night, easy taxis, good budget-to-mid choice.
    • Guilin Road · ≈ CNY 150–400 a night
      Student and Korean-food district; cheapest eats and a lively evening scene.
    • Hongqi Street · ≈ CNY 220–600 a night
      Quieter, greener area near the car industry district; handy if you have business there.

    Food & drink

    • Try Northeast barbecue (chuan'r) and guo bao rou, sweet-and-sour pork slices. Portions are big; share.
    • Dumplings and cold noodles are cheap and filling; a meal often costs ¥20–40.
    • Guilin Road has Korean and student food: stews, fried chicken, late-night snacks.
    • Winter markets sell frozen pears and tanghulu; eat them outside, they are meant to be cold.
    • Look for hot pot and iron-pot stews in winter. Book ahead on weekend evenings.

    Tips

    1. Visit the Museum of the Imperial Palace of Manchukuo for local history
    2. Bundle up heavily if visiting during the freezing winter months
    3. WeChat Pay and Alipay are absolutely essential
    4. Try local Dongbei cuisine like stewed dishes and dumplings
    5. Public transit is the most efficient way to navigate the urban sprawl
