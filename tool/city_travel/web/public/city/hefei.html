Getting around

Metro for most trips; taxis or Didi for short hops and late nights.

  • ✓Metro ★Lines 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 cover the city. Line 1 links both main train stations.
  • ✓BusBuses cost ¥1 non-air-conditioned or ¥2 air-conditioned. Routes are in Chinese only.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Green, blue and orange taxis start at ¥8; black taxis start at ¥9. Didi works well; have a Chinese number or Alipay set up.
  • ✓Rental carNot advised for short visits; traffic and parking are stressful.
  • ✓WalkingFine around the centre and parks in mild months. Use metro or taxis for longer hops.
  • ✓Bike-shareMobike and Hellobike cost ¥1–2 per ride. Needs WeChat or Alipay.

Climate

Humid subtropical: cold winters, hot humid summers, and the most rain in July. Spring and autumn are the best seasons.

  • 🌸Best time: April to May and September to OctoberHighs around 22–29°C with moderate rain. Ideal for parks, lakes and walking.
  • 🥵Hot, humid summer (June to August)Highs near 30–32°C and lows near 21–25°C. Muggy nights; do outdoor sights early or late.
  • 🌧️Wettest month is JulyAbout 16 rain days. Keep an umbrella and a backup indoor plan.
  • 🧥Cold winter (December to February)Highs of 8–11°C and lows near 0–1°C. Bring a warm coat; indoor heating can be limited.
  • ☂️Rain is spread through the yearMost months have 8–11 rain days. A small umbrella is worth packing any month.
  • 🧳What to packLight layers in spring and autumn, breathable clothes and sunscreen for summer, a warm coat for winter, and rain gear in July.

Landmarks 6

Must-see · 6

  1. 1

    Lord Bao Park

    The city's best-known sight, a leafy park built around the memorial to Bao Zheng, the incorruptible Song-dynasty official who is a folk hero across China. Come early morning to watch locals doing tai chi, and allow an hour or two to walk the grounds and the memorial halls.

  2. 2

    Sanhe Ancient Town

    A well-preserved water town of old lanes, stone bridges and traditional houses about an hour outside Hefei, the best place nearby to see Anhui's historic architecture. Go on a weekday to avoid crowds and wear comfortable shoes for the cobbled streets.

  3. 3

    Anhui Museum

    The leading museum in the province, strong on Anhui history, calligraphy and regional culture, and a good rainy-day option. It is free to enter with ID, but check opening days since many Chinese museums close on Mondays.

  4. 4

    Li Hongzhang Former Residence

    The family home of Li Hongzhang, the late-Qing statesman, giving a window into 19th-century official life in Hefei. It is compact, so pair it with a walk in the surrounding old streets.

  5. 5

    Xiaoyaojin Park

    A central park with a lake, gardens and a famous name from the Three Kingdoms era, popular with local families. Best in spring and autumn; it is an easy stroll from the city centre.

  6. 6

    Swan Lake

    A modern lakeside area with open views and a skyline backdrop, good for an evening walk. Go around sunset when the light is best and the waterfront is liveliest.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a day trip to Chao Lake, a large freshwater lake east of the city; check buses and timings on the day.
    • History fans can swap a city afternoon for the Three Kingdoms sites and Lord Bao's temple area.
    • Slow travellers can spend a morning walking Dashushan hill and an afternoon in the Anhui Museum.

    Getting around

    • Metro lines 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 cover the city; Line 1 links both main train stations.
    • Buses cost ¥1 non-air-conditioned or ¥2 air-conditioned; routes are in Chinese only.
    • Green, blue and orange taxis start at ¥8; black taxis start at ¥9.
    • Ride apps like Didi work well; have a Chinese number or Alipay set up.
    • Bike-share (Mobike, Hellobike) is cheap at ¥1-2 per ride; needs WeChat or Alipay.
    • Driving yourself is not advised for short visits; traffic and parking are stressful.

    Where to stay

    • City Centre · ≈ CNY 180–450 a night
      Walk to Bao Park, the Anhui Museum and the main shopping streets. Best for first-time sightseeing.
    • Swan Lake · ≈ CNY 250–600 a night
      Modern hotels around the lake and the Anhui TV tower. Quiet evenings, good for nature and views.
    • Hefei South Station · ≈ CNY 150–380 a night
      Handy for high-speed trains to Shanghai, Nanjing and Huangshan. Metro Line 1 reaches the centre.
    • Hefei Station · ≈ CNY 120–300 a night
      Older station north of the centre. Cheapest beds and easy Line 1 access; area is busier and plainer.

    Food & drink

    • Try Anhui cooking: braised pork, smoked and salted dishes, and bamboo shoots. It is savoury, not spicy.
    • Look for small local restaurants near Bao Park and the old lanes; menus are often Chinese only, so point or use a translation app.
    • Breakfast is cheap and local: steamed buns, congee and soy milk for a few yuan.
    • Night markets and food streets are best after dark; prices are low, roughly ¥10–40 a dish.
    • Big malls around Swan Lake and the centre have chain restaurants with picture menus if you want an easy first meal.

    Tips

    1. Known as one of China's fastest-growing tech hubs, particularly in quantum computing and EVs
    2. Visit Xiaoyaojin Park for a mix of Three Kingdoms history and modern leisure
    3. WeChat Pay and Alipay are mandatory for almost all transactions
    4. Try local Anhui cuisine, which emphasizes wild herbs, braising, and hearty stews
    5. The high-speed train connects it easily to Shanghai and Nanjing
