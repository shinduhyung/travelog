Getting around

Walk the centre; use the metro for the airport and longer hops.

  • ✓Metro ★Line 4 links the airport to the centre in about 40 minutes. Cheap and fast.
  • ✓BusBuses, trams and trolleybuses run 05:00–23:00, plus four night lines. A single ticket is about 1.60 лв; a day card about 4 лв.
  • ✓Taxi / ride appsTaxis are yellow. Use OK Supertrans or the Ridenow app to avoid overcharging.
  • ✗Rental carNot needed in the city; parking is hard. Rent only for trips outside Sofia.
  • ✓Walking ★The centre is walkable, but pavements are uneven and potholed. Watch your step.
  • ✓Contactless paymentBank cards and Apple/Google Pay work on metro gates and ticket readers.

Climate

Continental climate with four clear seasons: cold winters, warm dry summers and a wet spring.

  • ☀️Best time: May to SeptemberWarm days from 21°C to 28°C. Ideal for walking the city and hiking Vitosha.
  • 🥶Cold winters (December to February)Highs of 5–9°C and lows below freezing. Snow is possible. Pack a warm coat, hat and boots.
  • 🌧️Wettest in springMay has about 14 rain days. Showers pass quickly; carry a small umbrella.
  • 🌞Driest in late summerJuly, August and September have only 5–6 rain days. Reliable sunshine.
  • 🍂Mild, pleasant autumnSeptember and October stay at 18–24°C. Good light and fewer crowds.
  • 🧳What to packComfortable shoes for uneven pavements, layers for cool evenings, and a rain jacket in spring and autumn.

Landmarks 8

Must-see · 4

  1. 1

    Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, Sofia

    This gold-domed cathedral is Sofia's most recognizable symbol and a masterpiece of Balkan Orthodox architecture. Go early in the morning to avoid crowds and see the interior frescoes in quiet light; entry to the crypt museum is separate and modestly priced.

  2. 2

    Boyana Church

    A UNESCO World Heritage site famous for its 13th-century frescoes, considered precursors of the Renaissance. Visits are limited to 10 minutes inside to protect the art, so book a timed slot in advance and take bus 64 from the city center.

  3. 3

    Church of St George (Rotunda)

    A 4th-century Roman rotunda church tucked in a courtyard behind the Sheraton, with layers of medieval frescoes. It's free to enter and open daily; dress respectfully and avoid services.

  4. 4

    Vitosha Boulevard

    Sofia's main pedestrian artery, lined with shops, cafes and views of Vitosha Mountain. It's best at sunset when locals stroll; start from the Palace of Justice and walk toward the mountain.

    Swap in instead

    • Day trip to Rila Monastery: a UNESCO site in the mountains, roughly 2 hours each way. Check bus times on the day.
    • Add Plovdiv, a historic old town about 2 hours by bus or train, for a night or a long day.
    • Hike Vitosha mountain: take the cable car or a bus up and walk back down in a half day.

    Getting around

    • Metro Line 4 links the airport to the centre in about 40 min.
    • Buses, trams and trolleybuses run 05:00-23:00, plus four night lines.
    • A single ticket costs about 1.60 лв; a day card about 4 лв.
    • Contactless bank cards and Apple/Google Pay work on gates and readers.
    • Taxis are yellow; use OK Supertrans or the Ridenow app.
    • The centre is walkable, but pavements are uneven and potholed.

    Where to stay

    • City Centre · ≈ BGN 90–220 a night
      Walk to the cathedral, Roman ruins and the main sights. Best base for a first visit.
    • Vitosha Blvd · ≈ BGN 110–260 a night
      The main pedestrian street: cafés, shops and easy walking to everything central.
    • Lozenets · ≈ BGN 80–190 a night
      Quiet, leafy and local, with good metro and tram links to the centre.
    • Studentski Grad · ≈ BGN 50–120 a night
      Cheapest beds and lively student bars, but a metro or bus ride from the sights.

    Food & drink

    • Try Bulgarian classics: shopska salad, banitsa pastry and grilled meats at a mehana (tavern).
    • Breakfast on the go: a warm banitsa with ayran (yogurt drink) from a bakery counter, cheap and filling.
    • Look for lunch menus (obedno menu) at local restaurants; they cost far less than dinner.
    • Vegetarians are well served: shopska salad, roasted peppers and sirene (white cheese) dishes are everywhere.
    • Tipping is modest, around 10%. Check the bill; some places add a service charge.

    Tips

    1. The Cyrillic alphabet is used exclusively; learning a few letters will help with reading street signs
    2. Visit the stunning Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, one of the largest Eastern Orthodox cathedrals in the world
    3. You can literally step over ancient Roman ruins (Serdica) while walking through the Serdika metro station
    4. Vitosha Mountain is accessible via public transit and offers great hiking in summer and skiing in winter
    5. Nodding your head means 'no' and shaking it means 'yes' in traditional Bulgarian culture, which can be very confusing
