Getting around

Metro for most trips; Didi or taxi for late nights and the airport.

  • ✓Metro ★Five lines run about 07:00–22:00, fares ¥2–10. Ticket machines take paper cash only, so keep small notes.
  • ✓BusCity buses cost ¥2 and run roughly 06:00–22:00. A bus card cuts fares to about ¥0.90.
  • ✓Taxi / ride apps ★Taxis start at ¥8 plus a ¥3 fuel surcharge; insist on the meter. Didi works, and WeChat or Alipay are widely used to pay.
  • ✗Rental carNot needed for city sights. Use trains and buses for day trips instead.
  • ✓WalkingFine in the centre and parks. Distances grow outside downtown, so mix walking with the metro.
  • ✓Bikes / e-bikesHostels rent bikes for about ¥20 a day. Shared e-bikes cost about ¥2.5 for the first 20 minutes.
  • ✓Airport linkLine 6 links Changshui Airport to East Bus Station, then Line 3 into downtown.

Climate

Mild highland climate: cool, dry winters and a long, wet summer. Rarely hot, rarely freezing.

  • 🌤️Best time: October to AprilMild days around 15–24°C and fewer rainy days. Ideal for parks, temples and day trips.
  • 🌧️Wet summer (June to September)Rain on most days, up to 26 rain days in July and August. Showers often pass; carry rain gear.
  • 🧥Cool nights all yearLows drop to 4–8°C in winter. Even summer evenings are mild, so bring a light jacket.
  • ☀️Strong highland sunDays are bright even in the wet season. Use sunscreen and a hat at altitude.
  • 🍄Mushroom seasonWild mushrooms peak in the wet months. Eat them only at busy restaurants, always cooked.
  • 🧳What to packLayers, a rain jacket and waterproof shoes for summer; a warm coat for winter nights.

Landmarks 5

Must-see · 5

  1. 1

    Shilin Stone Forest

    A vast karst landscape of limestone pinnacles and pillars that is Kunming's most famous sight and a UNESCO-listed wonder. Go early in the morning to beat the tour buses, and wear sturdy shoes since the paths are uneven.

  2. 2

    Yuantong Temple

    Kunming's largest and oldest Buddhist temple, with a peaceful pond and halls set below street level. Dress modestly and keep your voice down; it is an active place of worship, and mornings are calmest.

  3. 3

    Dianchi Lake

    A huge lake on the city's edge, best known for the winter seagulls that flock to its shores and the views toward the Western Hills. Head out in the morning for clearer light and fewer crowds.

  4. 4

    Green Lake Park

    A lively central park with lotus ponds, bridges and locals dancing and playing music. It is free to enter and easy to reach on foot from the city center; late afternoon is the liveliest time.

  5. 5

    Yunnan Provincial Museum

    The best place to understand Yunnan's many ethnic groups and regional history. Check opening hours in advance as many Chinese museums close on Mondays, and allow a couple of hours.

    Swap in instead

    • Add a day for the Stone Forest (Shilin): buses and trains run from the city; check times on the day.

    • Take a side trip to a nearby old town or ethnic village; ask your hostel about day tours.

    Getting around

    • Metro has five lines, runs about 07:00-22:00, fares ¥2-10; ticket machines take paper cash only.
    • Line 6 links Changshui Airport to East Bus Station, then Line 3 into downtown.
    • City buses cost ¥2 and run roughly 06:00-22:00; a bus card cuts fares to about ¥0.90.
    • Taxis start at ¥8 plus a ¥3 fuel surcharge; insist on the meter.
    • Ride apps like Didi work; WeChat and Alipay are widely used for payments.
    • Bikes rent at hostels for about ¥20 a day; shared e-bikes cost about ¥2.5 for the first 20 min.

    Where to stay

    • City centre (Tangzixiang) · ≈ CNY 200–550 a night
      Walk to Green Lake Park, the old streets and markets. Metro nearby for trips out of town.
    • Kunming Railway Station area · ≈ CNY 150–400 a night
      Handy for early trains and buses onward. Busy, plain streets; good budget rooms.
    • Dianchi lakeside · ≈ CNY 250–700 a night
      Lake views, quieter evenings and easy access to the western hills. Far from the centre.
    • Kunming South Station area · ≈ CNY 180–450 a night
      Best for high-speed trains to other provinces. Little to see nearby; a sleep-and-go base.

    Food & drink

    • Try Crossing-the-Bridge rice noodles (过桥米线), the city's signature bowl. Expect roughly ¥20–40.
    • Wild mushrooms are a summer treat. Eat them only at busy, reputable restaurants and always cooked through.
    • Snack streets near the old town and university area sell grilled skewers, fried potatoes and flower cakes.
    • Flower cakes (鲜花饼) and Pu'er tea make easy, light gifts. Buy from shops, not only tourist stalls.
    • Street food is cheap, often ¥5–15 a snack. Carry small cash and use WeChat or Alipay where accepted.

    Tips

    1. Take it easy on your first day as the 1,900m altitude can cause mild fatigue
    2. WeChat Pay and Alipay are absolutely essential for all daily transactions
    3. Try 'Crossing the Bridge Noodles' (Guoqiao Mixian), the most famous regional dish
    4. Visit the nearby Stone Forest (Shilin) for striking karst limestone formations
    5. Enjoy the massive Dounan Flower Market, the largest fresh cut flower market in Asia
