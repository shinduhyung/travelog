Changes: schema normalized to this app; Natural Earth boundaries simplified with mapshaper (8% keep-shapes, precision 0.0001), except the original 30 countries which are preserved. Supplementary data uses geoBoundaries simplified geometry. Duplicate codes for new countries have distinct storage keys. Whole-area outlines are explicitly marked and are not counted as subdivisions. This combined file is a collection of datasets; underlying country datasets retain their respective licenses.
