## 선별 기준

- Existing metrics are excluded even if moved from country to city scope: internet, medical facilities, basic prices, traffic, liveability, GDP, safety, population totals and skyscraper counts.
- Do not repeat a metric in both country and city lists. Renewable energy usage and solar resource potential answer different questions; GDP is a denominator only for structural fiscal/economic indicators.
- Do not inflate counts with sex/age/sector splits, inverse ratios, total vs per-capita versions, or every disaster return period.
- Country national saving rate is not household bank-account ownership. Safe drinking-water access is not physical water-resource stress.
- City housing price/income measures purchasing housing; local purchasing power measures income adjusted for consumption prices.
- All retained candidates have >=10 distinct non-European numerical values for a common reference period; JRC periods can denote historical or modelled reference layers.
- Available numerical coverage is source coverage, not guaranteed matching to app city boundaries.
- Culture/sport, conference and startup additions are not proposed without ten verified numerical observations. Happiness figure fetch failed (403); conference counts not extracted; startup ranks alone were not treated as underlying values.
