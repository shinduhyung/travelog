- Burson 스포츠 도시 순위: Ranking numbers confirmed, but no public numerical score table was found. Rank alone was not counted as an underlying metric.
- NBA 구단 수·리그별 연고 도시 수: Overlaps existing sports-city collections; not new statistics.
